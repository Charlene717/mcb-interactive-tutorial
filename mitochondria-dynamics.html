粒線體動態與生質能

mtDNA、cristae 架構、fission/fusion、mitophagy、supercomplexes、mtDNA 疾病——細胞的「動態能量網路」。

mtDNA, cristae architecture, fission/fusion, mitophagy, supercomplexes, mtDNA diseases — the cell's dynamic energy grid.

概覽

遠不只能量工廠

粒線體是動態網路——持續 fission/fusion，移動於 MT 軌道上，與 ER、lysosome、LD 形成接觸位點 (Ch16)。其功能遠超 ATP 製造：

  • OXPHOS — ATP 合成 (~90% 細胞能量)；複合體 I-V + cyt c + 泛醌 (CoQ)
  • TCA cycle — 不只代謝樞紐，更是生物合成 hub (citrate → FA、αKG → aa、succinate → epigenetic 信號)
  • Ca²⁺ buffering — MCU 經 ER MAMs 攝取 Ca²⁺，調控細胞訊號
  • ROS signaling — 複合體 I/III 漏電子產 superoxide，低劑量為信號分子
  • Apoptosis — MOMP/cyt c 釋放 (Ch17)
  • 免疫 — mtDNA 進入細胞質 → cGAS-STING；MAVS 抗病毒平台
  • 內分泌 — 分泌 mitokines (FGF21, GDF15)、mitochondrial-derived peptides (humanin, MOTS-c)

Mitochondria are dynamic networks — continually undergoing fission/fusion, trafficking along MTs, contacting ER, lysosomes, and LDs (Ch16). Functions far exceed ATP production:

  • OXPHOS — ATP synthesis (~90% of cell energy); complexes I-V + cyt c + ubiquinone (CoQ)
  • TCA cycle — biosynthetic hub (citrate → FA, αKG → aa, succinate → epigenetic signaling)
  • Ca²⁺ buffering — MCU at ER MAMs, signal modulation
  • ROS signaling — complex I/III electron leak → superoxide; low doses = signals
  • Apoptosis — MOMP, cyt c release (Ch17)
  • Immunity — cytosolic mtDNA → cGAS-STING; MAVS antiviral platform
  • Endocrine — mitokines (FGF21, GDF15), mitochondrial-derived peptides (humanin, MOTS-c)
mtDNA

一、mtDNA 與 heteroplasmy

人類 mtDNA：16,569 bp 環狀，含 H 重股 + L 輕股，編碼 13 條 OXPHOS 蛋白（CI: 7 ND、CIII: cyt b、CIV: 3 COX、CV: ATP6/8）+ 22 tRNA + 2 rRNA (12S/16S)。D-loop 控制區 (~1.1 kb) 含轉錄起始與 OriH 複製起點；POLG 是唯一 mtDNA 聚合酶。

每個細胞含 100-10,000 個 mtDNA copy。Heteroplasmy = 同細胞含突變與野生型 mtDNA 共存；突變比例 (mutation load) 超過閾值才出現臨床症狀 (typically > 60-80%, 因組織而異)。卵母細胞發育時的 bottleneck 將 mtDNA 數量縮減至 ~10-200 copy 再重新擴增，使子代 mtDNA 多樣性快速漂變——這是母系遺傳與重組瘢痕的基礎。

2024 治療：① Mitochondrial Replacement Therapy (MRT)、英國已合法、首例兒已誕生；② DddA-derived Cytidine Base Editor (DdCBE) (Mougous & Liu 2020) 可在 mtDNA 進行 C→T 編輯，無需切斷雙鏈（與 CRISPR 不同；mtDNA 內 dsDNA cut 會被 degraded）；③ TALEN-mtDNA 切割可選擇性消除高比例突變 mtDNA。

Human mtDNA: 16,569 bp circular, H/L strands, encoding 13 OXPHOS proteins (CI: 7 ND, CIII: cyt b, CIV: 3 COX, CV: ATP6/8) + 22 tRNAs + 2 rRNAs (12S/16S). D-loop control region (~1.1 kb) holds transcription start and OriH replication origin; POLG is the sole mtDNA polymerase.

Each cell holds 100-10,000 mtDNA copies. Heteroplasmy = mutant and wild-type mtDNA coexist; clinical symptoms emerge once the mutation load exceeds a threshold (typically >60-80%, tissue-dependent). The oocyte bottleneck shrinks mtDNA to ~10-200 copies before re-expansion, driving rapid drift across generations — the basis of maternal inheritance.

2024 therapies: (1) Mitochondrial Replacement Therapy (MRT) — legal in UK, first babies born; (2) DddA-derived Cytidine Base Editor (DdCBE) (Mougous & Liu 2020) enables C→T editing in mtDNA without dsDNA breaks (unlike CRISPR; mtDNA dsBreaks are degraded); (3) TALEN-mtDNA cleavage can selectively eliminate high-load mutant mtDNA.

⚠️
經典 mtDNA 疾病： Leigh syndrome (multiple CI/CIV defects)；LHON (ND4 m.11778G>A，視神經病變)；MELAS (tRNA-Leu m.3243A>G，腦病變/中風發作)；MERRF (tRNA-Lys m.8344A>G)。常見 m.3243A>G 在普通人群 ~1/400，但多數為低 heteroplasmy。 Classic mtDNA diseases: Leigh syndrome (multi CI/CIV defects); LHON (ND4 m.11778G>A, optic neuropathy); MELAS (tRNA-Leu m.3243A>G, encephalopathy + stroke-like episodes); MERRF (tRNA-Lys m.8344A>G). m.3243A>G frequency ~1/400 in general population, mostly low heteroplasmy.
Fission / fusion

二、Fission / Fusion / Mitophagy

Fusion (融合)：外膜由 MFN1 / MFN2 同源 GTPase 配對；內膜由 OPA1（多種剪接型）介導。L-OPA1 (long) 必須維持以保持 cristae；OMA1 與 YME1L 在膜電位下降時切割 OPA1 → S-OPA1 → 觸發 fission。

Fission (分裂)：由 cytosolic DRP1 (dynamin-related) 由 receptors (Fis1, Mff, MiD49, MiD51) 招募至 OMM constriction site → DRP1 形成螺旋 → GTP 水解切斷膜。2023: Friedman 確認 ER tubule 預先「箝制」mitochondrion 為 fission 預備位點 (ER-mito contact)；INF2 + actin 提供初步收縮力。

Mitophagy (粒線體自噬)：選擇性清除損傷粒線體。

  • PINK1-Parkin pathway：膜電位 ΔΨ↓ → PINK1 累積於 OMM → 磷酸化 ubiquitin S65 與 Parkin → Parkin 活化 → 多泛素化 OMM 蛋白 → autophagy receptor (OPTN, NDP52) 招募 LC3 → 自噬
  • Receptor-mediated：BNIP3、NIX (BNIP3L)、FUNDC1 直接含 LIR motif，依組織與壓力類型啟動 (e.g., 缺氧)
  • Cardiolipin externalization：cardiolipin 由 IMM 翻到 OMM 後被 LC3 直接識別

Parkinson's disease: PINK1 或 Parkin loss-of-function 突變導致家族性 PD——dopaminergic 神經元粒線體質量管控失敗。

Fusion: OMM by MFN1/MFN2 homologous GTPase pairs; IMM by OPA1 (multiple isoforms). L-OPA1 must be maintained for cristae integrity; OMA1 and YME1L cleave OPA1 → S-OPA1 → triggers fission when ΔΨm drops.

Fission: cytosolic DRP1 recruited by OMM receptors (Fis1, Mff, MiD49, MiD51) → spiral assembly → GTP hydrolysis cuts. 2023: Friedman confirmed ER tubules pre-mark fission sites; INF2 + actin generates initial constriction.

Mitophagy: selective clearance of damaged mitochondria.

  • PINK1-Parkin: ΔΨm drop → PINK1 accumulates on OMM → phosphorylates ubiquitin S65 + Parkin → Parkin active → polyubiquitinates OMM proteins → receptors (OPTN, NDP52) recruit LC3 → autophagy
  • Receptor-mediated: BNIP3, NIX (BNIP3L), FUNDC1 carry LIR motifs; tissue/stress-specific (e.g., hypoxia)
  • Cardiolipin externalization: cardiolipin flips to OMM, directly recognized by LC3

Parkinson's disease: PINK1 or Parkin LoF → familial PD — dopaminergic neurons fail mitochondrial QC.

超複合體

三、Cristae 與 supercomplex

Cristae 為 IMM 凹陷的「能量隔室」，由 MICOS 複合體 (Mic60, Mic19, Mic10, Mic26, Mic27) 於 cristae junction 維持，並透過 OPA1 oligomer 鎖緊頸部，避免 cyt c 隨意逸出。Cristae 內 ΔpH 大、proton motive force (Δp) 高，是 ATP synthase 高效運作的關鍵。

OXPHOS supercomplexes (respirasome)：傳統「液態鑲嵌」模型認為複合體獨立漂浮——但 2016 後 CryoEM 確證形成有序 supercomplex：I+III₂+IV₁/₂。2024 Hirst 解析 mammalian respirasome 2.0 Å，揭示 CoQ 與 cyt c 移動的「locked channel」——這解釋 electron substrate channeling 與 ROS 減少。

ATP synthase (CV)：分子旋轉馬達。F₀ 質子流動驅動 c-ring (8-10 subunits in mammals) 旋轉 → 通過中心 γ-shaft 傳遞至 F₁ → 三個 β subunit 構象循環 (open/loose/tight) → 每轉合成 3 個 ATP。Boyer 1997 Nobel "binding change mechanism"。ATP synthase 二聚體在 cristae 尖端，使 IMM 彎曲形成 cristae 形狀本身。

Cristae are IMM invaginations — "energy compartments" maintained by the MICOS complex (Mic60, Mic19, Mic10, Mic26, Mic27) at cristae junctions, with OPA1 oligomers tightening the neck to prevent cyt c leakage. High ΔpH and proton motive force (Δp) inside cristae enable efficient ATP synthesis.

OXPHOS supercomplexes (respirasome): the old "fluid mosaic" view (independent complexes) is replaced; CryoEM since 2016 shows ordered supercomplexes I+III₂+IV₁/₂. Hirst (2024) solved mammalian respirasome at 2.0 Å, revealing a "locked channel" for CoQ/cyt c — explaining substrate channeling and lower ROS.

ATP synthase (CV): a molecular rotor. F₀ proton flow rotates the c-ring (8-10 subunits in mammals) → via the central γ-shaft → F₁ has three β subunits cycling (open/loose/tight) → 3 ATP per full turn. Boyer's 1997 Nobel "binding change". Dimerized ATP synthase at cristae ridges bends the IMM, sculpting cristae shape.

from skimage import filters, morphology

df = summarize(sk)
print("Mean branch length (μm):", df.branch_distance.mean()*0.108)
print("# branches:", len(df))
print("Mitochondrial volume fraction:", binary.sum()/binary.size)
