📌 教學註記與細節

本教程力求採用 2022–2024 最新成果，但分子細胞生物學進展迅速。下列為已知須留意的部分。

This tutorial strives to reflect the 2022–2024 state of the art, but molecular cell biology evolves rapidly. Items to keep in mind:

補充說明 · Notes

① AlphaFold 與動態構象

AlphaFold2/3 預測的是單一靜態構象，不直接反映折疊景觀的多態性或 allosteric switching；IDR 的「低 pLDDT」應解讀為「動態」而非「預測失敗」（參考 Lane 2023 Nat Struct Mol Biol）。

AlphaFold2/3 predicts a single static conformation; it does not capture conformational ensembles or allosteric switching. Low pLDDT in IDRs should be read as "dynamic" rather than "prediction failure" (see Lane 2023, Nat Struct Mol Biol).

補充說明 · Notes

② 凝聚物的「液態」描述須謹慎

早期 LLPS 文獻將許多 membraneless organelle 一律描述為「液滴」；近年 super-resolution 與 single-molecule 研究顯示，許多體外的「液滴」在細胞內呈現 sub-saturation cluster、network 或 nanocluster 形態（參考 Lyon 2024 Nat Rev MCB、Mittag 系列）。「LLPS」應更廣義理解為 "biomolecular condensation"。

Early LLPS papers labeled many membraneless organelles as "droplets". Recent super-resolution and single-molecule work shows that many in-vitro droplets exist in cells as sub-saturation clusters, networks or nanoclusters (Lyon 2024 Nat Rev MCB, Mittag series). "LLPS" is better understood as the broader concept of "biomolecular condensation".

補充說明 · Notes

③ Cuproptosis 仍在發展中

Tsvetkov 等 2022 提出 cuproptosis，但 NCCD 尚未正式將其納入 nomenclature（截至 2024）；機制是否獨立於 ferroptosis/apoptosis、是否需要 caspase 等仍有討論空間。本教程歸類為「emerging modality」。

Cuproptosis was defined by Tsvetkov et al. 2022, but the NCCD has not yet officially incorporated it into its nomenclature (as of 2024). Whether it is mechanistically independent from ferroptosis/apoptosis remains debated. This tutorial categorizes it as an "emerging modality".

補充說明 · Notes

④ Yamanaka 部分重編程的老化逆轉尚未在人體確證

Belmonte (2016)、Sinclair (2020) 在小鼠展示部分重編程可改善老化表型，但人體臨床試驗甫展開；DNA methylation clock 的「年齡」與生理年齡的因果關係仍是研究議題。請以「假設」而非「結論」看待。

Belmonte (2016) and Sinclair (2020) showed that partial reprogramming improves aging phenotypes in mice, but human clinical trials have only just begun. The causal link between methylation-clock "age" and physiological age is still under investigation — treat as hypothesis, not conclusion.

補充說明 · Notes

⑥ GPCR 不是「兩態開關」

早期教科書常將 GPCR 描述為「活化／非活化」兩態，但 single-molecule FRET 與 cryo-EM 已顯示同一受體可採取多個中間構象，且不同 ligand（agonist、partial agonist、biased agonist、inverse agonist）穩定不同子集，對應 G-protein 與 β-arrestin pathway 的偏好性（biased signaling）。請參考 Wingler & Lefkowitz 2020 Annu Rev Pharmacol Toxicol 與 Wingler 2020 Cell。教學中「活化／非活化」應視為簡化模型。

Textbooks often portray GPCRs as binary "active/inactive" switches, but single-molecule FRET and cryo-EM show that a single receptor samples multiple intermediate conformations, with different ligand classes (agonist, partial agonist, biased agonist, inverse agonist) stabilizing distinct sub-ensembles that bias G-protein vs β-arrestin signaling (Wingler & Lefkowitz 2020 ARPT; Wingler 2020 Cell). Treat the two-state cartoon as a teaching simplification.

⑦ 持續 OSKM 表現會致瘤

與 ④（部分重編程對老化的假設）不同，這是已被確證的現象：在小鼠體內持續表現 OSKM 會誘發 dedifferentiated teratoma 與多器官畸胎瘤，因此 in vivo reprogramming 必須使用 pulsed / cyclic / inducible 策略（doxycycline-off）以避免去分化失控。請參考 Abad et al. 2013 Nature 與 Ocampo et al. 2016 Cell。「Yamanaka 因子可逆轉老化」不等於「可長期安全表現」。

Distinct from ④ (the aging-reversal hypothesis), this is well established: sustained OSKM expression in mice induces dedifferentiated teratomas and multi-organ tumors, so in-vivo reprogramming requires pulsed/cyclic/inducible regimes (e.g., doxycycline-off) to avoid uncontrolled dedifferentiation (Abad 2013 Nature; Ocampo 2016 Cell). "Yamanaka factors can rejuvenate" is not the same as "can be safely expressed long-term".

⑧ 看到凝聚物不等於凝聚物有功能

與 ② 不同（②談「液態」描述的物理準確性），這一點談因果：許多 LLPS 文獻僅顯示「存在凝聚物」便宣稱該凝聚物驅動下游功能，但近年研究指出，許多體內 puncta 是 over-expression artifact、是上游訊號的旁觀者、或其消失並不影響下游活性。建議採用「凝聚物形成 vs 凝聚物功能」分離測試（如 IDR 突變、optogenetic Cry2-mediated 控制 condensate 但不改變蛋白濃度）。請參考 McSwiggen 2019 Genes Dev 與 Düster 2021 Nat Struct Mol Biol。

Distinct from ② (which concerns the physical accuracy of "liquid" language), this is a causation issue: many LLPS papers infer downstream function from the mere existence of a condensate, but recent work shows that many cellular puncta are over-expression artifacts, passive bystanders, or dispensable for the proposed activity. Use separation-of-function tests (IDR mutants, optogenetic Cry2 modules that toggle condensation without changing concentration) (McSwiggen 2019 Genes Dev; Düster 2021 NSMB).

⑨ 非典型自噬 LAP / CASM

教學常將「ATG-LC3 lipidation = canonical autophagy」，但 LC3 也會經由 V-ATPase–ATG16L1 axis 鎖定到單膜（single-membrane）結構，稱為 CASM（Conjugation of ATG8 to Single Membranes）；LAP（LC3-associated phagocytosis）是其代表。CASM 不形成 double-membrane autophagosome、不需要 ULK1/FIP200、卻仍使用 ATG12-ATG5-ATG16L1 與 ATG8。這在病原體吞噬、efferocytosis 與藥物誘導 (如 monensin、chloroquine) 中皆出現。請參考 Vargas 2023 Nat Rev MCB 與 Hooper 2022。判斷 autophagy flux 不能只看 LC3 puncta。

"ATG–LC3 lipidation = canonical autophagy" is an oversimplification. LC3 can also be conjugated to single membranes via the V-ATPase–ATG16L1 axis (CASM, Conjugation of ATG8 to Single Membranes), of which LAP is the prototype. CASM does not form double-membrane autophagosomes, does not require ULK1/FIP200, yet still uses the ATG12-ATG5-ATG16L1 and ATG8 machinery — it operates in pathogen engulfment, efferocytosis, and drug-induced contexts (monensin, chloroquine). See Vargas 2023 Nat Rev MCB. LC3 puncta alone do not measure autophagic flux.

⑩ 諾貝爾獎與「最新進展」的時差

教學引用「Nobel-awarded」時應留意時差：Kobilka & Lefkowitz 的 GPCR 結構工作在 2007 年（β2-AR crystal）達到關鍵突破，2012 年才獲頒 Nobel Chemistry；Karikó & Weissman 的 modified-nucleoside mRNA 關鍵論文在 2005 Immunity，但 2023 Physiology or Medicine 才獲獎；Charpentier & Doudna 的 CRISPR-Cas9 2012 PNAS / Science，2020 才獲獎。故「最新 Nobel」≠「最新科學」，引用時建議標註「原始發表年」與「獲獎年」兩者。

When citing "Nobel-awarded" work, note the typical 6–18 year lag: Kobilka & Lefkowitz's β2-AR crystal structure landed in 2007, the Chemistry Nobel in 2012; Karikó & Weissman's modified-nucleoside mRNA paper appeared in Immunity 2005 but the Medicine Nobel was awarded only in 2023; Charpentier & Doudna's CRISPR-Cas9 (PNAS/Science 2012) was honored in 2020. "Latest Nobel" ≠ "latest science" — cite both the original publication year and the award year.

⑪ AlphaFold 對 IDP / 寡聚體 / 配體的限制

與 ① 不同（①談「靜態構象」），這一點談具體適用範圍：(a) Intrinsically disordered proteins/regions 因為本就沒有單一結構，AF2 pLDDT 低不是「失敗」也不是「動態」，而是「不適用」；(b) AlphaFold-Multimer 對 transient / weak / 異源寡聚體準確度顯著下降，尤其抗體–抗原界面與膜蛋白組裝；(c) AlphaFold3（2024 Nature）雖支援核酸、配體、PTM，但對 ligand 結合模式的成功率（~76% in PoseBusters benchmark）仍低於 dedicated docking 工具，且非開源/受限商用授權。請參考 Bryant 2023 Nat Struct Mol Biol 與 Abramson 2024 Nature。生產用途仍需濕實驗或正交方法驗證。

Distinct from ① (which is about static vs dynamic structure), this concerns scope: (a) IDPs/IDRs have no single structure, so a low AF2 pLDDT here means "not applicable", not "failed" or "dynamic"; (b) AlphaFold-Multimer accuracy drops sharply for transient/weak/heteromeric assemblies, especially antibody–antigen interfaces and membrane-protein complexes; (c) AlphaFold3 (Nature 2024) adds nucleic acids, ligands, and PTMs but its ligand-pose success (~76% in the PoseBusters benchmark) still trails dedicated docking, and it is not open source under permissive license (Bryant 2023 NSMB; Abramson 2024 Nature). Production use still requires wet-lab or orthogonal validation.

⑫ UPR ≠ ISR；別忽略 RIDD 與 ATF6 家族

教學常把「PERK–eIF2α 磷酸化」直接與「UPR」劃等號，但 eIF2α phosphorylation 屬於更大的 Integrated Stress Response（ISR），由 PERK、PKR、GCN2、HRI 四種 kinase 共同匯聚。UPR 還包含 IRE1α（XBP1 splicing 與 RIDD — Regulated IRE1-Dependent Decay）與 ATF6（含 ATF6α、ATF6β 及 CREB3/CREB3L1–4 等 type-II membrane bZIP 家族成員）。RIDD 直接降解選擇性 mRNA（含 miRNA 前體），不是透過轉錄因子。請參考 Costa-Mattioli & Walter 2020 Science 與 Hetz 2020 Nat Rev MCB。藥物開發須區分 ISRIB（針對 eIF2B）與 IRE1/PERK kinase 抑制劑的不同效應。

Equating "PERK–eIF2α phosphorylation" with "the UPR" is misleading. eIF2α phosphorylation belongs to the broader Integrated Stress Response (ISR), driven by four kinases (PERK, PKR, GCN2, HRI). The UPR also includes IRE1α — with XBP1 splicing and RIDD (Regulated IRE1-Dependent Decay of selected mRNAs and miRNA precursors) — and the ATF6 arm (ATF6α, ATF6β and the related CREB3/CREB3L1–4 type-II membrane bZIP family). Drug development must distinguish ISRIB (which targets eIF2B) from IRE1/PERK kinase inhibitors (Costa-Mattioli & Walter 2020 Science; Hetz 2020 Nat Rev MCB).
