機械轉導：細胞如何「感覺」力

從 Piezo 三葉螺旋槳到 YAP/TAZ 核轉位——力學訊號如何被翻譯為化學與基因表達語言。

From Piezo's three-blade propeller to YAP/TAZ nuclear translocation — how mechanical signals are translated into chemical and transcriptional language.

概覽

力學訊號的三條路徑

細胞並非被動的化學容器，而是持續感應並回應來自 ECM 硬度、流體剪應力、細胞間張力、滲透壓的機械輸入。2010 年 Patapoutian 實驗室發現 Piezo1/Piezo2（2021 Nobel）開啟了機械感受 (mechanosensation) 的分子時代，今日機械轉導已成為癌症 invasion、幹細胞分化、組織形態發生的核心議題。

三條經典力傳感路徑：① 機械敏感離子通道（Piezo、TRP、TREK、TMC）秒以下時程；② integrin-focal adhesion 將 ECM 力學耦合至 actin；③ LINC complex 將胞漿張力傳遞至核內染色質。

Cells are not passive chemical containers — they continuously sense and respond to mechanical inputs from ECM stiffness, fluid shear, intercellular tension, and osmotic pressure. The 2010 discovery of Piezo1/Piezo2 by Patapoutian (Nobel 2021) opened the molecular era of mechanosensation. Today mechanotransduction is central to cancer invasion, stem-cell differentiation, and tissue morphogenesis.

Three canonical force-sensing routes: (1) mechanosensitive ion channels (Piezo, TRP, TREK, TMC), sub-second timescale; (2) integrin-focal adhesion coupling ECM force to actin; (3) LINC complex transmitting cytoplasmic tension to nuclear chromatin.

💡
核心比喻：細胞像彈簧網路。力學輸入會改變這張網的張力分布——蛋白構象、酵素活性、轉錄因子定位都隨之重設。Tensional homeostasis 是當代力學生物學的中央概念。 Core metaphor: A cell is a spring network. Mechanical input redistributes tension — protein conformations, enzyme activities, and transcription factor localization all reset. Tensional homeostasis is a central concept in modern mechanobiology.
機械通道

一、Piezo1/2 三葉螺旋槳

Piezo1 為 三聚體 (trimer)，每單體約 2,500 殘基，總質量 > 900 kDa。CryoEM (Ward et al., 2018; Lin et al., 2022) 顯示其呈三葉螺旋槳 (three-blade propeller) 構象——每片 blade 由 9 個 THU (transmembrane helical unit) 堆疊而成，整體形成 dome 構型，使局部膜呈 ~10 nm 深的凹陷。當膜張力增加 (~1.4 mN/m)，dome 趨於扁平，扭轉中央 CTD (C-terminal domain) 並開啟 pore。

新進展 (2023-2024): Cox 實驗室與 Coste 顯示 Piezo1 inactivation 動力學受 PI(4,5)P₂ 與 cholesterol 調控；STOML3 與 MyoTubularin 等 raft 蛋白可降低活化閾值。Piezo2 的 G2756X loss-of-function 突變導致 distal arthrogryposis；Piezo1 突變則造成 hereditary xerocytosis（脫水溶血）。

Piezo1 is a trimer; each subunit has ~2,500 residues, total mass >900 kDa. CryoEM (Ward et al., 2018; Lin et al., 2022) reveals a three-blade propeller — each blade contains 9 transmembrane helical units (THUs) stacked into a dome that bends the local membrane into a ~10 nm bowl. Increasing membrane tension (~1.4 mN/m) flattens the dome, twisting the central CTD to open the pore.

Recent (2023-2024): Cox & Coste labs show Piezo1 inactivation kinetics are tuned by PI(4,5)P₂ and cholesterol; raft proteins STOML3 and MyoTubularin lower the activation threshold. Piezo2 G2756X loss-of-function causes distal arthrogryposis; Piezo1 mutations cause hereditary xerocytosis (dehydrating hemolysis).

二、Integrin–Talin–Vinculin 力傳感

Focal adhesion (FA) 是細胞與 ECM 的力學介面。Integrin αβ 異二聚體外側結合 fibronectin/collagen，內側招募 talin——talin 的 R3 螺旋束在 ~5 pN 力下展開，暴露 11 個 vinculin binding sites (VBS)，招募 vinculin 並進一步耦合至 F-actin。這是經典的force-induced unfolding/binding。

Integrin 與 fibronectin 的鍵屬catch bond——力越大鍵越穩（與 slip bond 相反），這允許 FA 在拉力下穩定而非斷裂。FA 的成熟由 ~25 種蛋白組成 (paxillin, FAK, p130Cas, zyxin)，其中 p130Cas 的 substrate domain 在拉伸下暴露 Tyr 位點被 Src 磷酸化，是訊號放大關鍵。

Focal adhesion (FA) is the mechanical interface between cell and ECM. Integrin αβ heterodimers bind ECM (fibronectin, collagen) extracellularly and recruit talin intracellularly. Talin's R3 helix bundle unfolds at ~5 pN, exposing 11 vinculin-binding sites (VBSs) that recruit vinculin and couple to F-actin — classic force-induced unfolding/binding.

Integrin–fibronectin is a catch bond — force strengthens the bond (opposite of slip bond), keeping FAs stable under load. Mature FAs contain ~25 proteins (paxillin, FAK, p130Cas, zyxin); the p130Cas substrate domain stretches to expose Tyr sites for Src phosphorylation, a key amplification step.

硬度感應

三、YAP/TAZ 與 ECM 硬度

當 ECM 硬度從 soft (1 kPa, 腦) 增加到 stiff (40 kPa, 骨前)，actomyosin 張力 (NMII-pMLC) 上升，FA 成熟，RhoA-ROCK 軸活化。下游 Hippo pathway 中的 MST1/2-LATS1/2 激酶被抑制——LATS1/2 對 YAP 的 S127 磷酸化減少，YAP 失去 14-3-3 細胞質錨定，進入細胞核與 TEAD1-4 共活化 CTGF, CYR61, ANKRD1 等基因——驅動增殖、抗失巢凋亡 (anoikis)、EMT。

Engler 2006 經典實驗：MSC 在 1 kPa 凝膠分化為神經元；10 kPa → 肌肉；40 kPa → 骨——硬度單獨即可決定譜系。

As ECM stiffness rises from soft (1 kPa, brain) to stiff (40 kPa, pre-bone), actomyosin tension (NMII-pMLC) increases, FAs mature, and RhoA-ROCK activates. Downstream Hippo kinases MST1/2-LATS1/2 are inhibited — LATS-mediated YAP S127 phosphorylation drops, YAP loses 14-3-3 cytosolic anchoring and enters the nucleus, where it co-activates CTGF, CYR61, ANKRD1 via TEAD1-4 — driving proliferation, anoikis resistance, EMT.

Engler 2006 classic: MSCs on 1 kPa gel → neurons; 10 kPa → muscle; 40 kPa → bone. Stiffness alone determines lineage.

機械核

四、LINC complex 與機械核

LINC (Linker of Nucleoskeleton and Cytoskeleton) 是橫跨核外膜 (ONM) 與核內膜 (INM) 的力學橋樑。ONM 上的 Nesprin (KASH domain) 與胞漿 actin/MT/IF 連接，其 KASH 尾穿過核周圍腔 (perinuclear space) 與 INM 的 SUN1/2 (三聚體) 結合，SUN N 端再與 lamin A/C 網狀核纖層接觸。

當胞漿張力增加，LINC 將力直接傳至核膜使核變形——這會 (1) 拉開 NPC、加速核質運輸；(2) 改變 LADs (lamina-associated domains) 與 lamin 的接觸，重塑異染色質；(3) 活化 cPLA2 在核膜釋放 AA→eicosanoids。Lammerding (2023): 細胞穿越 <3 μm 孔時核變形可導致核膜破裂、cGAS 進入並啟動先天免疫；同時 DNA 損傷增加（migrasome）。

LINC (Linker of Nucleoskeleton and Cytoskeleton) spans ONM and INM as a mechanical bridge. ONM Nesprins (KASH) link to cytoplasmic actin/MT/IF; their KASH tail crosses the perinuclear space to bind SUN1/2 trimers on INM, whose N-termini contact the lamin A/C meshwork.

Rising cytosolic tension transmits via LINC to deform the nucleus — (1) stretching NPCs accelerates nucleocytoplasmic transport; (2) altering LAD–lamin contacts remodels heterochromatin; (3) activating cPLA2 at the NE releases AA → eicosanoids. Lammerding (2023): cells squeezing through <3 μm pores can rupture the NE, allowing cGAS entry and innate-immune activation; DNA damage accumulates.

⛔
Laminopathies: Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria 由 LMNA C661T 產生 progerin（缺失 50 aa, 殘留 farnesyl），使核變脆且機械敏感性增加，外源剪應力導致細胞快速衰老——機械轉導疾病的範例（Ch13 詳述）。 Laminopathies: Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria — LMNA C661T produces progerin (50-aa deletion, retained farnesyl). Nuclei become fragile and hyper-mechanosensitive; external shear accelerates senescence — a paradigm mechanotransduction disease (detailed in Ch13).

五、量測力學工作流

# Traction Force Microscopy — pyTFM (Bauer 2021)
from pyTFM.TFM_functions import calculate_deformation, TFM_tractions
import numpy as np
from skimage import io

ref = io.imread("beads_relaxed.tif")
act = io.imread("beads_active.tif")
# 1) PIV-based deformation field
u, v, mask = calculate_deformation(act, ref, window_size=64, overlap=32)
# 2) Fourier-Transform Traction Cytometry (FTTC)
tx, ty = TFM_tractions(u, v,
    pixelsize_pix=0.108,         # μm/pixel
    young=10000,                # Pa (substrate E)
    sigma=0.49, h=300,           # Poisson, gel thickness μm
    lambda_2=1e-10)             # regularization
# 3) 總牽引力 (nN)
mag = np.sqrt(tx**2 + ty**2)
print("Total traction:", mag.sum() * (0.108e-6)**2 * 1e9, "nN")
# YAP nuclear/cytoplasmic ratio quantification (CellProfiler+R)
library(tidyverse); library(ggpubr)
df <- read_csv("yap_intensities.csv")  # cols: cell_id, nuc_int, cyt_int, stiffness
df <- df %>% mutate(NC_ratio = nuc_int / cyt_int,
                  stiff_bin = cut(stiffness, breaks=c(0,1,10,40),
                                   labels=c("soft","med","stiff")))
# ANOVA + Tukey HSD
fit <- aov(NC_ratio ~ stiff_bin, data=df)
TukeyHSD(fit)
ggviolin(df, x="stiff_bin", y="NC_ratio", fill="stiff_bin") +
  stat_compare_means(comparisons=list(c("soft","stiff")))
