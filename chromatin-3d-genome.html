染色質與 3D 基因組

從 147 bp 核小體到 megabase TAD——histone code、cohesin loop extrusion 與 A/B compartments 如何在三個維度組織基因表達。

From 147 bp nucleosome to megabase TAD — how the histone code, cohesin loop extrusion, and A/B compartments organize gene expression in three dimensions.

概覽

3D 基因組的層級

每個人類細胞核（直徑 ~10 µm）內塞著 2 公尺的 DNA，這要求多尺度組織：

  • 核小體 (10 nm)：147 bp DNA 纏繞 H2A-H2B-H3-H4 八聚體 1.65 圈
  • 染色質纖維 (30 nm)：傳統「珠鏈」模型，但活細胞中存在性受質疑——可能更接近液態高分子刷
  • 染色質環 (50–500 kb)：cohesin loop extrusion，被 CTCF 邊界錨定
  • TAD (Topologically Associating Domain, ~1 Mb)：高頻接觸的功能域
  • A/B compartments (~5 Mb)：A=活躍/開放、B=異染色質/封閉
  • 染色體領地 (chromosome territory)：每條染色體在核內占據相對固定區域

Each human nucleus (~10 µm diameter) packs 2 metres of DNA — requiring multiscale organization:

  • Nucleosome (10 nm): 147 bp DNA wrapping the H2A-H2B-H3-H4 octamer 1.65 times
  • 30 nm fibre: classical "beads-on-string" model — existence in live cells contested; likely closer to a liquid polymer brush
  • Chromatin loops (50–500 kb): cohesin loop extrusion, anchored by CTCF boundaries
  • TAD (~1 Mb): topologically associating domain — functional contact unit
  • A/B compartments (~5 Mb): A=active/open, B=heterochromatin/closed
  • Chromosome territories: each chromosome occupies a relatively fixed region
💡
當代轉折：2017 年 Ou et al. 的 ChromEMT 顯示活細胞中染色質不形成固定 30 nm 纖維；2023 後共識是染色質為動態、不規則折疊的高分子，其組織由 active forces (loop extrusion, transcription, condensate formation) 持續維持。 Modern shift: 2017 ChromEMT (Ou et al.) showed no fixed 30 nm fibre in live cells. The 2023+ consensus: chromatin is a dynamic, irregularly folded polymer, organized by active forces (loop extrusion, transcription, condensates).

環擠出

三、Cohesin Loop Extrusion

2017-2019 的單分子實驗（Davidson, Kim et al.）直接證明 cohesin 是 ATP-依賴的 DNA-motor，能將 DNA 擠成擴大的環。模型如下：

  1. NIPBL-MAU2 將 cohesin (SMC1-SMC3-RAD21-STAG1/2) 裝載到 DNA
  2. Cohesin 以 ~0.5-2 kb/s 速率擠出 loop（雙向）
  3. 遇到「收斂 (convergent)」方向的 CTCF 時被卡住（CTCF N-term 與 cohesin SA 結合）
  4. WAPL 與 PDS5 從 DNA 上釋放 cohesin（負調控）
  5. 結果：在 CTCF-CTCF 對之間形成 50-500 kb 的染色質環，定義 TAD 邊界

2017-2019 single-molecule experiments (Davidson, Kim et al.) directly demonstrated cohesin is an ATP-dependent DNA motor extruding loops. Model:

  1. NIPBL-MAU2 loads cohesin (SMC1-SMC3-RAD21-STAG1/2) onto DNA
  2. Cohesin extrudes loops bidirectionally at ~0.5-2 kb/s
  3. Stops at convergent CTCF sites (CTCF N-term binds cohesin SA)
  4. WAPL + PDS5 unload cohesin (negative regulator)
  5. Result: 50-500 kb chromatin loops between CTCF-CTCF pairs, defining TAD boundaries
⛔
TAD 邊界破壞的疾病後果：CTCF 結合位點的單核苷酸變異或結構變異 (SV) 可能將遠端 enhancer 「劫持 (hijack)」到不適當的基因，例如 EPHA4 locus 的 SV 重新分配 enhancer，導致肢體畸形 (Lupiáñez et al. 2015)。腦瘤中 IDH 突變 → 5hmC ↑ → CTCF 結合丟失 → PDGFRA enhancer hijack。 Disease consequences of TAD boundary disruption: SNVs or structural variants at CTCF sites can "hijack" distal enhancers to inappropriate genes — e.g., EPHA4 locus SVs cause limb malformations (Lupiáñez et al. 2015). In glioma, IDH-mutant 5hmC↑ → CTCF binding loss → PDGFRA enhancer hijacking.

X 失活與 Polycomb

五、Xist / Polycomb

Xist lncRNA（約 17 kb）由失活 X 染色體單獨表達，是已知最大的順式 (cis) 作用 ncRNA。它從 Xic locus 起始，「塗布 (coat)」整條 X，依序招募：

  1. SPEN（透過 A-repeat）→ HDAC3 → 去乙醯化
  2. hnRNPK（透過 B/C-repeat）→ PRC1.6 → H2AK119ub
  3. PRC2 被 H2AK119ub 招募 → H3K27me3 廣泛沉積
  4. SMCHD1、macroH2A 進一步穩定異染色質
  5. 形成失活 X (Xi) — 整條染色體濃縮成 Barr body

2023-2024 揭示 Xist 形成多個 LLPS 域，每個域內維持局部沉默；這是「凝聚物機制」在染色質沉默的關鍵案例。

Xist lncRNA (~17 kb) is exclusively expressed from the inactive X — the largest known cis-acting ncRNA. From the Xic, it coats the entire X, sequentially recruiting:

  1. SPEN (via A-repeat) → HDAC3 → deacetylation
  2. hnRNPK (via B/C-repeat) → PRC1.6 → H2AK119ub
  3. PRC2 recruited by H2AK119ub → broad H3K27me3
  4. SMCHD1, macroH2A stabilize heterochromatin
  5. Inactive X (Xi) condenses into the Barr body

2023-2024 work shows Xist forms multiple LLPS domains, each maintaining local silencing — a key example of condensate-mediated chromatin silencing.
