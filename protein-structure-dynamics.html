蛋白質結構與動態

從折疊景觀到 AlphaFold3——理解蛋白質的能量地景、內在無序區與分子動力學如何在原子尺度推動細胞功能。

From folding landscapes to AlphaFold3 — how energy surfaces, intrinsically disordered regions, and molecular dynamics drive cell function at atomic scale.

概覽

為什麼「動態」比「結構」更關鍵？

傳統 X-ray 結構給我們的是「一張靜照」，但蛋白質的功能來自於其能量地景上的構象集合 (conformational ensemble)。每個折疊好的蛋白質在生理溫度下會在多個低能態間漲落 (fluctuate)，這些動態（從 ps 級的鍵振動到 ms 級的構象切換）才是酵素催化、訊號傳導、變構調節的真正基礎。

2024 年的 AlphaFold3 與 RoseTTAFold All-Atom 不僅能預測單體結構，還能預測蛋白-核酸-小分子-離子的複合體結構與動態構象。配合 CryoEM 與 CryoET 的原位結構解析，今日的分子細胞生物學已進入「原子尺度活體成像visual proteomics — 在細胞內以 nm 級解析觀察蛋白質複合體的時間動態」時代。

X-ray crystallography gives "a single snapshot", but protein function arises from a conformational ensemble on its energy landscape. At physiological temperature, every folded protein fluctuates among multiple low-energy states. These dynamics — from ps-scale bond vibrations to ms-scale conformational switching — underlie enzyme catalysis, signal transduction, and allosteric regulation.

The 2024 release of AlphaFold3 and RoseTTAFold All-Atom can now predict protein-nucleic acid-ligand-ion complexes and dynamic conformations. Combined with CryoEM and CryoET in-situ imaging, molecular cell biology has entered the era of atomic-scale live imagingvisual proteomics — nm-resolution observation of protein complex dynamics inside intact cells.

💡
核心原則：結構 → 動態 → 功能。任何只看靜態結構的解釋都是不完整的；要解釋酵素變構、訊號開關、藥物選擇性，必須考慮構象漲落 (conformational fluctuation)。 Core principle: Structure → Dynamics → Function. Static structure alone is insufficient. Explaining allostery, signaling switches, and drug selectivity requires considering conformational fluctuations.
折疊景觀

一、能量漏斗與分子動力學

Levinthal 悖論指出：若蛋白以隨機方式搜索構象空間，所需時間將超過宇宙年齡。實際上，蛋白質的折疊路徑由能量漏斗 (energy funnel)引導——序列同時編碼了路徑與終點。

Levinthal's paradox: random search through conformation space would exceed the age of the universe. In reality, folding is guided by an energy funnel — sequence encodes both the path and the destination.

🔻

折疊漏斗

多條路徑、單一原生態。Chevron 圖反映折疊/解折疊速率對變性劑的依賴。

Many paths, one native state. Chevron plots show folding/unfolding rate dependence on denaturant.

🌊

局部極小

動力學陷阱 (kinetic trap) 形成錯誤折疊，與 prion、Alzheimer Aβ 等聚集疾病相關。

Kinetic traps cause misfolding, linked to prion disease, Alzheimer's Aβ, and other aggregation disorders.

🎲

構象漲落

在原生態盆地內振盪，週期 ps → ms。NMR relaxation 與 single-molecule FRET 可量化。

Oscillation within the native basin, ps → ms timescales. Quantified by NMR relaxation and smFRET.

⚠️
變構 (allostery) 的當代觀點：不再是「兩態切換」，而是「構象集合的分佈重組」。受體被配體佔據後，原本的構象比例被重新調整——這對藥物設計（偏選性 agonism）至關重要。 Modern view of allostery: Not "two-state switching" but reweighting of the conformational ensemble. Ligand binding shifts the population of pre-existing states — critical for drug design (biased agonism).
內在無序區

二、內在無序蛋白 (IDP) 與 IDR

真核蛋白約 30% 含內在無序區 (Intrinsically Disordered Region, IDR)，無固定結構卻具特定功能：作為訊號通路的中樞、相分離的驅動者、轉錄因子的活化域。它們富含 Pro、Gly、Ser、Glu、Lys 等極性/帶電殘基，缺乏疏水核心。

2024 後新焦點：AlphaFold 對 IDR 預測信心 (pLDDT) 偏低不是缺陷，反而是發現 IDR 的線索。結合 IDRome 與 NARDINI+ 工具能識別 IDR 的序列文法 (sequence grammar)，預測其是否傾向相分離。

~30% of eukaryotic proteins contain Intrinsically Disordered Regions (IDRs) — no fixed structure but specific functions: signaling hubs, phase separation drivers, transcription factor activation domains. They are rich in Pro, Gly, Ser, Glu, Lys and lack a hydrophobic core.

Post-2024 focus: Low AlphaFold confidence (pLDDT) over IDRs is a feature, not a bug — it flags candidate IDRs. IDRome and NARDINI+ identify the sequence grammar of IDRs and predict phase-separation propensity.

三、結構動態的計算工作流

從 AlphaFold3 預測到 MD 動力學模擬的完整工作流。R 端以 bio3d 處理結構分析；Python 端以 biotite + OpenMM 進行模擬。

From AlphaFold3 prediction to MD simulation. R workflow uses bio3d; Python uses biotite + OpenMM.

# 載入 PDB 與結構動態分析
library(bio3d)
pdb <- read.pdb("1AKE")             # adenylate kinase open form
# 1) PCA on ensemble (NMA 簡正模態分析)
modes <- nma(pdb)
plot(modes, sse=pdb)
# 2) RMSF profile — 識別柔性區域
rmsf_val <- rmsf(modes$U.subspace)
# 3) 比對 open vs closed conformations
pdbs <- pdbaln(c("1AKE","4AKE"), fit=TRUE)
pc <- pca(pdbs$xyz, rm.gaps=TRUE)
plot(pc, col=c("open","closed"))
# OpenMM MD 模擬 (隱式溶劑, 100 ns)
from openmm.app import *
from openmm import *
from openmm.unit import *

pdb = PDBFile('protein.pdb')
ff  = ForceField('amber14-all.xml', 'amber14/tip3pfb.xml')
system = ff.createSystem(pdb.topology, nonbondedMethod=PME,
                         nonbondedCutoff=1.0*nanometer, constraints=HBonds)
integrator = LangevinMiddleIntegrator(310*kelvin, 1/picosecond, 2*femtoseconds)
sim = Simulation(pdb.topology, system, integrator)
sim.context.setPositions(pdb.positions)
sim.minimizeEnergy()
sim.reporters.append(DCDReporter('traj.dcd', 5000))
sim.step(50_000_000)   # 100 ns @ 2 fs/step
# AlphaFold3 / ColabFold via local Docker (2024+)
# 1) 製備輸入 (FASTA 或 mmCIF)
cat > input.json <<EOF
{
  "name": "my_complex",
  "sequences": [
    {"proteinChain": {"sequence": "MKT...", "count": 2}},
    {"ligand": {"ligand": "ATP", "count": 1}}
  ]
}
EOF
# 2) 跑 AlphaFold3
docker run --gpus all -v $(pwd):/data alphafold3:latest \
  --json_path=/data/input.json --output_dir=/data/out
# 3) 評估指標
#   - pTM > 0.5: 整體結構可信
#   - ipTM > 0.6: 介面預測可信
#   - pLDDT < 50: 可能為 IDR/柔性區
⛔
AF3 限制：① 對於 obligate complexes 表現好，但 transient/weak interactions 仍易失誤。② 對於蛋白-RNA 介面預測仍有限。③ pLDDT 高 ≠ 真實結構；需 CryoEM/SAXS 實驗驗證。 AF3 limitations: ① Good on obligate complexes, but transient/weak interactions remain unreliable. ② Protein-RNA interface prediction is still limited. ③ High pLDDT ≠ true structure; experimental validation via CryoEM/SAXS is needed.

四、實驗 vs 計算技術選擇

🌳 我該用哪種方法解析我的蛋白？

Q1→想要單一原生狀態的原子解析結構？→ X-ray (結晶) 或 CryoEM (>50 kDa, single particle)Need atomic-resolution static structure? → X-ray (crystallizable) or CryoEM (>50 kDa, single particle)
Q2→在細胞內的原位結構與排列？→ CryoET + subtomogram averagingNeed in-situ structure inside cells? → CryoET + subtomogram averaging
Q3→想知道 ps–ms 動態與構象漲落？→ NMR (<30 kDa) 或 HDX-MSWant ps–ms dynamics? → NMR (<30 kDa) or HDX-MS
Q4→單分子尺度構象轉換？→ smFRET / optical tweezers / AFMSingle-molecule conformational transitions? → smFRET / optical tweezers / AFM
Q5→沒有實驗資源、想要快速初步預測？→ AlphaFold3 + ESMFold（注意 IDR 區段 pLDDT < 50 的訊號）No experimental access, want fast initial prediction? → AlphaFold3 + ESMFold (note pLDDT < 50 over IDRs)
Q6→有結構但想模擬原子層級動力學？→ Classical MD (OpenMM / GROMACS) 或 enhanced sampling (REMD, metadynamics)Have structure, want atomistic dynamics? → Classical MD (OpenMM/GROMACS) or enhanced sampling (REMD, metadynamics)

自我檢核（3 題）

1. 一段蛋白序列在 AlphaFold3 預測中 pLDDT 持續低於 50，最可能的解釋為何？

1. A protein region has AlphaFold3 pLDDT consistently below 50. What is the most likely explanation?

A. 序列搜尋失敗，模型缺乏訓練資料A. Sequence search failed; model lacked training data
B. 蛋白絕對折疊失敗，無法表達B. Protein cannot fold at all; will not express
C. 該區段為內在無序區 (IDR)，無單一原生態C. The region is intrinsically disordered (IDR), with no single native state
D. 蛋白已突變為非功能性D. Protein has mutated to non-functional state
✔ C：低 pLDDT 是 IDR 的指標，配合 NARDINI+ 等工具可進一步分析其序列文法。
✔ C: Low pLDDT flags candidate IDRs. Tools like NARDINI+ can further analyze sequence grammar.

2. 關於變構 (allostery) 的「構象集合」觀點，下列何者最正確？

2. Regarding the "conformational ensemble" view of allostery, which is most correct?

A. 配體誘導蛋白由 A 構象切換到 B 構象A. Ligand induces switching from conformation A to B
B. 配體未結合前蛋白已具多種構象，結合後僅是重新分布各構象比例B. Multiple conformations pre-exist; ligand binding reweights their populations
C. 變構僅發生在二聚體蛋白C. Allostery only occurs in dimeric proteins
D. 變構效應在生理溫度下不存在D. Allosteric effects do not exist at physiological temperature
✔ B：當代觀點認為構象先存在，配體只是 reweight 集合分佈——這對偏選性 agonism 設計至關重要。
✔ B: Modern view — conformations pre-exist; ligands reweight the ensemble. Crucial for biased agonist design.
