生物分子凝聚物與 LLPS

無膜胞器的物理學——multivalent 弱交互作用如何驅動相分離、形成功能性凝聚物，並在神經退化中走向病態固化。

The physics of membraneless organelles — how multivalent weak interactions drive phase separation, form functional condensates, and undergo pathological hardening in neurodegeneration.

概覽

無膜胞器的物理

2009 年 Brangwynne 與 Hyman 觀察到秀麗線蟲 P granules 像液滴一樣融合、剪切、彈回球形，揭示了無膜胞器本質：液-液相分離 (Liquid-Liquid Phase Separation, LLPS)。從那時起，細胞內超過 30 種「凝聚物 (condensates)」被定義——皆為由多價弱交互作用驅動、與周圍細胞質保持動態平衡的液態次相 (sub-phase)。

核心物理為 Flory-Huggins 理論：當蛋白濃度超過 saturation concentration (Csat)，且溫度低於 critical temperature (Tcrit)，系統會分裂為「dense phase (凝聚物內)」與「dilute phase (細胞質)」。LLPS 不只是「結晶 vs 溶液」的二元選擇，而是 phase diagram 上連續的相轉變。

In 2009, Brangwynne & Hyman observed C. elegans P granules fusing, shearing, and rebounding into spheres like liquid droplets — revealing the essence of membraneless organelles: Liquid-Liquid Phase Separation (LLPS). Since then, 30+ cellular "condensates" have been defined — liquid sub-phases driven by multivalent weak interactions, in dynamic equilibrium with the surrounding cytoplasm.

Core physics: Flory-Huggins theory. When protein concentration exceeds saturation concentration (Csat) and T < Tcrit, the system separates into "dense phase" (inside condensate) and "dilute phase" (cytoplasm). LLPS is not binary crystal-vs-solution but a continuous phase transition on the phase diagram.

💡
驅動力 = multivalency：需要多個弱相互作用位點同時存在。經典模式為「stickers and spacers」——IDR 中的芳香殘基 (Y, F, W)、帶電殘基簇、SH3-PRM 重複、RNA-binding motifs 都可作為 sticker。 Driving force = multivalency: Multiple weak interaction sites must coexist. Classic "stickers and spacers" model — aromatics (Y, F, W), charge patches, SH3-PRM repeats, RNA-binding motifs in IDRs act as stickers.

病態

二、凝聚物老化與神經退化

液 → 凝膠 → 固體的轉變是凝聚物的「老化 (aging)」現象。FUS、TDP-43、hnRNPA1都含 prion-like domain (PrLD)，正常情形下進入應力顆粒短暫存在；但ALS/FTD 病人突變（FUS R495X、TDP-43 G298S、hnRNPA1 D262V）使凝聚物無法解體，逐漸固化為 amyloid-like fibrils，導致神經元死亡。

2023-2024 的關鍵進展：RNA 比例決定凝聚物物性——低 RNA 時 FUS 凝聚物易老化；高 RNA 維持液態。這解釋為何核內 (RNA 豐富) FUS 為液態，細胞質應力顆粒 (RNA 稀少) 易老化。

The liquid → gel → solid transition is condensate "aging". FUS, TDP-43, hnRNPA1 all carry prion-like domains (PrLD) and normally pass through stress granules transiently. But ALS/FTD mutations (FUS R495X, TDP-43 G298S, hnRNPA1 D262V) prevent disassembly, leading to amyloid-like fibrils and neuronal death.

Key 2023-2024 finding: RNA stoichiometry dictates material properties — low RNA promotes FUS aging; high RNA maintains liquid state. This explains why nuclear FUS (RNA-rich) is liquid while cytoplasmic stress granules (RNA-poor) age.

PTM 調控

三、PTM 調控 LLPS

細胞透過 PTM 動態地改寫蛋白的「相分離傾向」，等同於對 IDR 的序列文法進行即時編輯：

Cells dynamically rewrite phase-separation propensity via PTMs — real-time editing of IDR sequence grammar:

  • 磷酸化 (Ser/Thr/Tyr)：增加負電 → 破壞 electrostatic stickers (如 FUS RGG)；多位點磷酸化可溶解凝聚物（細胞週期關閉核仁的機制）
  • Phosphorylation: adds negative charge → disrupts electrostatic stickers (e.g. FUS RGG); multi-site phospho can dissolve condensates (used to disassemble nucleolus during mitosis)
  • Arg 甲基化 (PRMT1/5)：減弱 cation-π → 抑制 FUS LLPS；ALS 風險因子
  • Arg methylation (PRMT1/5): weakens cation-π → inhibits FUS LLPS; ALS risk factor
  • SUMOylation / ubiquitination：SUMO 提供 multivalent SIM-SUMO 介面（PML body 核心驅動力）；K63-Ub 鏈促 NEMO 凝聚物
  • SUMOylation / ubiquitination: SUMO provides multivalent SIM-SUMO interface (PML body driver); K63-Ub chains promote NEMO condensates
  • O-GlcNAc：取代磷酸化位點，常為「促進凝聚物」之反向訊號
  • O-GlcNAc: competes with phosphorylation; usually pro-condensate
