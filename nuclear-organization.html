核組織與核孔

~30 nucleoporins 組成的城門、Ran-GTP 動力、lamin 網狀核纖層——核如何同時封閉又敞開？

~30 nucleoporins forming the gate, Ran-GTP energetics, lamin meshwork — how the nucleus stays both closed and open at once.

概覽

核的四層架構

核膜由外核膜 (ONM)與內核膜 (INM)組成。ONM 連續於粗糙 ER，是膜蛋白合成與脂質供應通路；INM 富含 lamin 接觸蛋白 (LBR, LAP2β, emerin, MAN1)；兩膜在 NPC 處融合，核周間隙 ~50 nm。

核內部呈現高度非隨機組織：

  • 染色質區域 (Chromosome territories)：每條染色體佔據獨立空間 (Cremer)；基因密集染色體 (19, 17) 靠核中心；基因稀疏 (18, X-inactive) 靠核纖層
  • LADs (Lamina-Associated Domains)：~1,300 LAD 平均 0.5 Mb，富含異染色質 (H3K9me2/3)，是 lamin B 與 PRR14 招募的目標
  • 核小體 (Nucleolus)：rRNA 轉錄/加工/組裝中心；FC + DFC + GC 三層結構
  • 其他無膜次核體：Cajal、PML、splicing speckle、paraspeckle、HLB (histone locus body)

The NE is a double membrane: ONM (continuous with rough ER, supplies lipid and TM proteins) and INM (enriched in lamin-binding proteins LBR, LAP2β, emerin, MAN1). The two fuse at NPCs; perinuclear space ~50 nm.

The interior is highly non-random:

  • Chromosome territories (Cremer): each chromosome occupies a distinct domain; gene-dense (19, 17) sit centrally, gene-poor (18, inactive X) at the lamina
  • LADs: ~1,300 domains, mean 0.5 Mb, H3K9me2/3-rich heterochromatin, tethered by lamin B and PRR14
  • Nucleolus: rRNA transcription/processing/assembly hub; FC + DFC + GC tri-layer
  • Membraneless bodies: Cajal, PML, splicing speckles, paraspeckles, HLB
核孔複合體

一、NPC 結構與選擇性

NPC 是真核細胞最大的蛋白質複合體（~110 MDa, ~1,000 個 nucleoporins (Nups)，~30 種類型，8 重對稱）。2022 由 Beck / Hoelz / Schwartz 三組獨立發表的近原子解析度結構整合了 X-ray、CryoEM、CryoET、AlphaFold 預測，揭示完整架構：

  • Cytoplasmic ring (CR)：8 個 Y-shaped Nup107-160 複合體 + Nup358/RanBP2 細胞質絲
  • Inner ring (IR)：Nup93、Nup205、Nup188 等，是 scaffold 主體
  • Nuclear ring (NR)：Y-complex 鏡像 + Nup50, ELYS
  • Nuclear basket：TPR、Nup153 構成籃狀向核質延伸
  • FG-Nup 中央通道：~200 FG-rich IDR，形成選擇性凝聚相——疏水 FG 重複互動建立屏障，僅允許 importin/exportin 包裹的貨物穿越

2024 進展：Hoelz 解析 NPC 在 mitotic NE breakdown 時的解構順序——cyclin B-CDK1 磷酸化 Nup98 觸發解離，這也是癌細胞 chromatin 損傷敏感性的重要節點。

The NPC is the largest eukaryotic protein complex (~110 MDa, ~1,000 Nups, ~30 types, 8-fold symmetric). The 2022 near-atomic structures from Beck / Hoelz / Schwartz integrated X-ray, CryoEM, CryoET, and AlphaFold, revealing:

  • Cytoplasmic ring (CR): 8 Y-shaped Nup107-160 complexes + Nup358/RanBP2 fibrils
  • Inner ring (IR): Nup93, Nup205, Nup188 — the scaffold body
  • Nuclear ring (NR): mirror Y-complex + Nup50, ELYS
  • Nuclear basket: TPR, Nup153 extending nucleoplasm-side
  • FG-Nup central channel: ~200 FG-rich IDRs forming a selective phase — hydrophobic FG interactions create a barrier permeable only to importin/exportin-cargoed traffic

2024: Hoelz lab resolved NPC disassembly during mitotic NE breakdown — cyclin B-CDK1 phosphorylates Nup98, triggering dissolution. Key node for cancer chromatin-damage sensitivity.

💡
NPC 與 LLPS：FG-Nup IDR 是經典 LLPS 系統——in vitro 重構 hydrogel 可選擇性招募 importin。Frey/Görlich 2007 開創 selective phase model；2023 Lemke 實驗室證實 FG-Nup condensates 在活細胞中亦呈相分離行為。 NPC and LLPS: FG-Nup IDRs are a classic LLPS system — reconstituted hydrogels in vitro selectively recruit importins (Frey/Görlich 2007 selective-phase model). Lemke (2023) confirmed FG-Nup condensates phase-separate in live cells.
Ran 系統

二、Ran 系統

Ran GTPase 的核質不對稱是核質運輸的能量基礎：核內 Ran-GTP 高（因 RCC1/GEF 駐於染色質）；質內 Ran-GDP 高（因 RanGAP1 駐於細胞質側 NPC fibrils）。

Import 循環：① cargo-NLS 與 importin-α 結合，importin-α 與 importin-β 結合 → 進入核 → ② Ran-GTP 結合 importin-β 使其釋放 cargo → ③ importin-β/Ran-GTP 出核 → ④ 細胞質側 RanGAP 水解 GTP → importin-β 釋放、重新使用。

Export 循環：cargo-NES 與 CRM1/XPO1 在 Ran-GTP 存在下形成複合體 → 出核 → 質內 RanGAP 水解 GTP → 釋放 cargo。Selinexor (KPT-330) 為 XPO1 抑制劑，2019 FDA 核准用於 multiple myeloma；機制為阻斷腫瘤抑制因子 (p53, FOXO, IκB) 的核外運輸——讓它們留在核內發揮抑制作用。

Ran GTPase asymmetry powers transport: nuclear Ran-GTP is high (RCC1/GEF on chromatin); cytoplasmic Ran-GDP is high (RanGAP1 on cytoplasmic NPC fibrils).

Import cycle: (1) NLS-cargo binds importin-α; importin-α binds importin-β → enter nucleus → (2) Ran-GTP binds importin-β, releasing cargo → (3) importin-β/Ran-GTP exits → (4) cytoplasmic RanGAP hydrolyzes GTP → importin-β recycles.

Export cycle: NES-cargo binds CRM1/XPO1 with Ran-GTP → exits → cytoplasmic RanGAP → cargo released. Selinexor (KPT-330) — XPO1 inhibitor, FDA-approved 2019 for multiple myeloma — blocks nuclear export of tumor suppressors (p53, FOXO, IκB), retaining them where they can suppress oncogenesis.

核纖層

三、核纖層與 LADs

核纖層 (nuclear lamina) 是 INM 內側 ~10-20 nm 的 type-V 中間絲網狀層，由 lamin A/C (LMNA) 與 lamin B1/B2 (LMNB1/2) 組成。A/B 形成獨立但交織的網狀結構——A 較動態、B 較靜態。Lamin A 前驅物 prelamin A 經 farnesylation → 切除 → 成熟 lamin A；progerin 缺少最後切割步驟，殘留 farnesyl 卡在膜上。

LADs：~30-40% 基因組與 lamina 接觸，DamID 技術 (van Steensel) 是繪製 LADs 的金標準。LADs 富含 H3K9me2/me3 異染色質，PRR14 為連接器。Mitosis 時 LADs 重置；G1 早期重新建立 (~99% 維持，但仍有「LAD 漂移」變化基因表達)。

The nuclear lamina is a ~10-20 nm type-V IF meshwork on the INM, built from lamin A/C (LMNA) and lamin B1/B2 (LMNB1/2). A and B form independent but interwoven networks — A is dynamic, B is static. Prelamin A is farnesylated and cleaved to mature lamin A; progerin lacks the final cleavage step, leaving a farnesyl that traps it at the membrane.

LADs: ~30-40% of the genome contacts lamina; DamID (van Steensel) is the gold standard for mapping. LADs are H3K9me2/3-rich heterochromatin; PRR14 is a tether. LADs reset during mitosis and reestablish in early G1 (~99% maintained, with measurable "LAD drift" shifting expression).
