神經退化共通機制

二、神經退化共通機制

🧠

Alzheimer's

Aβ 斑塊 + tau NFTs。APP 經 β-/γ-secretase 切出 Aβ42 → 寡聚毒性 → tau 過磷酸化 (AT8)。療法：Lecanemab/Donanemab（抗 Aβ-protofibrils，2023-2024 FDA 核准）；BACE/γ-sec 抑制劑多失敗；tau 抗體在試。

Aβ plaques + tau NFTs. APP cleaved by β-/γ-secretase → Aβ42 → toxic oligomers → tau hyperphosphorylation (AT8). Therapies: Lecanemab/Donanemab (anti-Aβ protofibrils, FDA 2023-24); BACE/γ-sec inhibitors mostly failed; tau antibodies in trials.

🌀

Parkinson's

α-synuclein Lewy body + 黑質多巴胺神經元喪失。LRRK2/GBA/PINK1/Parkin 突變家族型。療法：levodopa；GBA1 modulator (venglustat 失敗)；α-syn 抗體 prasinezumab；GDNF 基因療法；GFP-iPSC dopaminergic 神經元移植 (Studer 2024 phase 1)。

α-synuclein Lewy body + nigral dopaminergic loss. Familial: LRRK2/GBA/PINK1/Parkin. Tx: levodopa; GBA1 modulators (venglustat failed); α-syn antibodies prasinezumab; GDNF gene therapy; iPSC-derived dopaminergic transplant (Studer 2024 phase 1).

🧬

ALS / Huntington's

ALS：TDP-43/FUS LLPS 失調、C9orf72 GGGGCC 六核苷酸擴展 → RAN 翻譯 DPR；療法 Tofersen (ASO-SOD1, 2023 FDA)。HD：HTT CAG 擴展 → polyQ aggregate；Tominersen/branaplam ASO 試驗（前者 2021 失敗，重新設計中）。

ALS: TDP-43/FUS LLPS dysregulation; C9orf72 GGGGCC hexanucleotide expansion → RAN-translated DPRs. Tx: Tofersen (ASO-SOD1, FDA 2023). HD: HTT CAG expansion → polyQ aggregates; Tominersen/branaplam ASO trials (2021 failure, redesign).

跨疾病共通機制：① 蛋白質聚集 + 蛋白穩態失常（Ch8）；② 失能自噬（Ch18）；③ 粒線體功能異常（Ch15）；④ neuroinflammation（小膠細胞 NLRP3）；⑤ 異常凝聚物（Ch4 RNA-binding protein LLPS）；⑥ 突觸功能損傷。整合多元治療（如 ASO + senolytics + autophagy enhancer）為新方向。

Shared mechanisms across disorders: ① protein aggregation + proteostasis collapse (Ch8); ② disabled autophagy (Ch18); ③ mitochondrial dysfunction (Ch15); ④ neuroinflammation (NLRP3 microglia); ⑤ aberrant condensates (Ch4 RBP LLPS); ⑥ synaptic deficits. Combinatorial therapy (ASO + senolytic + autophagy enhancer) is an emerging direction.
