膜生物物理與脂質組學

不只是「液態鑲嵌」——脂筏、脂質不對稱、機械性質與 PI 訊號脂質如何重塑當代膜生物學。

Beyond "fluid mosaic" — rafts, asymmetry, mechanics, and PI signaling lipids reshaping modern membrane biology.

當代觀點

從 1972 模型到今日

Singer-Nicolson fluid mosaic 模型開啟了膜生物學，但今天我們知道生物膜遠比一張流動的脂質海更複雜：

  • 橫向異質性 (lateral heterogeneity)：膽固醇 + sphingolipid 富集的「脂筏 (lipid rafts)」直徑 10-200 nm，動態存在
  • 跨層不對稱 (transbilayer asymmetry)：外層富 PC/SM；內層富 PS/PE/PI——由 flippase/floppase/scramblase 維持
  • 機械性質：彎曲剛度 (κ_b)、表面張力、線張力——決定 vesicle 形成、cell shape、機械感應
  • 脂質訊號：PI(4,5)P₂、DAG、Ceramide、S1P 是訊號分子，不只是結構元件

The Singer-Nicolson fluid mosaic model launched membrane biology, but today we know membranes are far more complex than a flowing lipid sea:

  • Lateral heterogeneity: cholesterol/sphingolipid-rich lipid rafts 10–200 nm, dynamic
  • Transbilayer asymmetry: outer leaflet PC/SM; inner PS/PE/PI — maintained by flippase/floppase/scramblase
  • Mechanics: bending rigidity (κ_b), surface tension, line tension — drive vesicle formation, cell shape, mechanosensing
  • Lipid signaling: PI(4,5)P₂, DAG, ceramide, S1P are signals, not just structural
💡
PS 外翻訊號：正常細胞 PS 嚴格保留在內層；apoptosis 時 PS 外翻成為 "eat-me" 訊號（給巨噬細胞識別）。這是膜不對稱在程序性死亡的關鍵應用（→ Ch17）。 PS externalization signal: PS is strictly inner-leaflet in healthy cells; during apoptosis it flips outward as an "eat-me" signal for macrophages — a key application of membrane asymmetry in programmed death (→ Ch17).

磷脂醯肌醇訊號

二、PI 訊號脂質

七種磷脂醯肌醇 (PtdIns) 由不同位置 (3,4,5) 磷酸化形成，分布於不同細胞器，作為「身分郵戳」吸引特定蛋白：

Seven phosphoinositides (PtdIns) form by phosphorylation at positions 3/4/5, distributing across organelles as "identity stamps" recruiting specific proteins:

PI(4,5)P₂ (PIP2)

細胞膜內層。經 PLC 切為 IP₃ + DAG；招募 ezrin/radixin/moesin；actin 動力學關鍵。

Plasma membrane inner leaflet. Cleaved by PLC → IP₃ + DAG; recruits ezrin/radixin/moesin; key for actin dynamics.

PI(3,4,5)P₃ (PIP3)

PI3K 產物；招募 AKT、PDK1（PH domain）。PTEN 為其拮抗劑。PI3K/AKT 通路在癌症關鍵 (Ch9)。

PI3K product; recruits AKT, PDK1 (PH domain). Antagonized by PTEN. PI3K/AKT axis is pivotal in cancer (Ch9).

PI3P

Early endosome / phagophore (自噬). FYVE / PX domain 識別。自噬 (Ch18) 的關鍵脂質。

Early endosome / phagophore. Recognized by FYVE/PX domains. Key lipid in autophagy (Ch18).

PI4P

Golgi / TGN；與 OSBP 介導 cholesterol 反向運輸。MAMs 接觸點重要 (Ch16)。

Golgi/TGN; OSBP-mediated countertransport of cholesterol. Important at MAMs (Ch16).

PI(3,5)P₂

Late endosome / lysosome；TRPML1 通道調控；溶酶體 Ca²⁺ 釋放關鍵。

Late endosome/lysosome; regulates TRPML1 channels; key for lysosomal Ca²⁺ release.

PI(3,4)P₂

Macropinosome、recycling endosome；SHIP 磷酸酶產物，AKT 部分活化。

Macropinosomes, recycling endosomes; SHIP phosphatase product; partial AKT activation.
