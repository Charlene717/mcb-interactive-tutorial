mRNA 生命週期與表觀轉錄體

從 capping、剪接、輸出到 NMD 與 codon optimality——加上 m6A 表觀轉錄體如何重新定義「mRNA 是什麼」。

From capping, splicing, and export to NMD and codon optimality — plus how m6A epitranscriptomics redefines what an mRNA is.

概覽

mRNA 的五大關卡

從 Pol II 轉錄到核糖體上的功能 mRNA，每分子要通過至少五道品管：

  1. 5′ Capping：m7G + 2′-O-methyl，新生轉錄 25-30 nt 即發生
  2. Co-transcriptional splicing：U1/U2/U4/U5/U6 spliceosome 在 Pol II 延伸時切除內含子
  3. 3′ end processing：CPSF 切位 + ~200 A polyadenylation
  4. Export：TREX (THO + Aly/Ref) 招募 Nxf1-Nxt1 異二聚體穿越 NPC
  5. Decay quality control：NMD、no-go decay、non-stop decay、CCR4-NOT 去 polyA

每一道都附加 PTM 與 RNA modification（m6A、m5C、ψ、Cap1 m6Am）——構成表觀轉錄體 (epitranscriptome)，這是 2010 後最大的 RNA 生物學變革。

From Pol II nascent RNA to functional mRNA, each molecule passes at least five QC gates:

  1. 5′ Capping: m7G + 2′-O-methyl, after ~25-30 nt of transcription
  2. Co-transcriptional splicing: U1/U2/U4/U5/U6 spliceosome removes introns during elongation
  3. 3′ end processing: CPSF cleavage + ~200 A polyadenylation
  4. Export: TREX (THO + Aly/Ref) recruits Nxf1-Nxt1 heterodimer to cross the NPC
  5. Decay QC: NMD, no-go decay, non-stop decay, CCR4-NOT deadenylation

Each step layers PTMs and RNA modifications (m6A, m5C, ψ, Cap1 m6Am), forming the epitranscriptome — the biggest RNA-biology shift since 2010.

💡
耦合原則：capping、splicing、3′-end processing 都耦合於 Pol II CTD（Ch6）。CTD 修飾切換決定哪些 mRNA 加工因子被招募——這是「轉錄與 mRNA 加工不可分」的分子基礎。 Coupling principle: capping, splicing, 3′-processing are all coupled to Pol II CTD (Ch6). CTD phospho-state determines which processing factor is recruited — the molecular basis of "transcription and mRNA processing are inseparable".
剪接

一、剪接體動力學

真核 pre-mRNA 約 90% 包含內含子，需被剪除——每個內含子由5 個 snRNP（U1, U2, U4, U5, U6）+ ~150 個輔因子組成的剪接體切除。整個循環經歷多個構象，每步 ATP 依賴：

~90% of eukaryotic pre-mRNAs contain introns, removed by spliceosomes built from 5 snRNPs (U1, U2, U4, U5, U6) + ~150 cofactors. The catalytic cycle involves multiple ATP-dependent conformational states:

A complexU2 取代 SF1 結合 BP (ATP)BP commit
B complexU4/U6.U5 tri-snRNP 加入前催化
BactBRR2 解開 U4-U6；PRP8 活化活化

C* / P complex第二次轉酯：exon-exon 連接切除 intron
ILS / disassemblyPrp22 釋放 mRNA；intron 被 debranching enzyme 處理解體

SF3B1 (U2 factor) is recurrently mutated in MDS, CLL, uveal melanoma (K700E hotspot), causing cryptic 3′SS usage and aberrant mRNAs. SF3B1i (H3B-8800) is in trials. U1 snRNA mutation (r.3a>g) occurs in SHH-medulloblastoma and CLL.
m6A 表觀轉錄體

二、m6A 表觀轉錄體

N6-methyladenosine (m6A) 是 mRNA 上最豐富的修飾——每條 mRNA 含 1-3 個 m6A（共識序列 DRACH），主要在 3′ UTR 與 stop codon 附近。系統由「writer-eraser-reader」組成：

N6-methyladenosine (m6A) is the most abundant mRNA modification — 1-3 per mRNA at DRACH consensus motifs, enriched near stop codons and in 3′ UTRs. A writer-eraser-reader system:

角色蛋白功能
寫入METTL3-METTL14 + WTAP, VIRMA, ZC3H13, HAKAI沉積 m6A
移除FTO, ALKBH5去甲基
讀取YTHDF1/2/3, YTHDC1/2命運決定
讀取IGF2BP1/2/3, HNRNPA2B1穩定化
⛔
2022-2024 修正：傳統「YTHDF1=轉譯，YTHDF2=降解」分工被質疑——Lan, He et al. 顯示三者高度冗餘，主要共同促進降解。最新模型：YTHDF1/2/3 是 m6A-mRNA 流向 P-body/decay 凝聚物的聚合驅動者。FDA 已有 METTL3i (STC-15) 進入 phase I AML 治療。 2022-2024 revision: The classical YTHDF1=translation, YTHDF2=decay split is questioned — Lan, He et al. show high redundancy, mostly driving decay together. New model: YTHDF1/2/3 are condensation drivers moving m6A-mRNA into P-body/decay condensates. METTL3i (STC-15) is in phase I AML trials.

降解

三、降解與 codon optimality

NMD (Nonsense-Mediated Decay)：核心邏輯是「stop codon 後方還剩 EJC (Exon Junction Complex) ≥ 50-55 nt」即觸發降解：

  1. Ribosome 終止於 PTC（premature stop）
  2. SURF complex 形成：SMG1 (PIKK kinase) + UPF1 + eRF1 + eRF3
  3. SMG1 磷酸化 UPF1 → DECID 複合體
  4. SMG6 (endonuclease) 切割 mRNA；SMG5-SMG7 招募 CCR4-NOT 與 DCP1/2
  5. XRN1 與 exosome 完成降解

Codon optimality (Coller 2014+)：mRNA 半衰期受其密碼子組成影響——常用密碼子（高 tRNA 豐度）→ 翻譯流暢 → mRNA 穩定；罕用密碼子 → ribosome 停頓 → CCR4-NOT 招募 → deadenylation → 降解。這解釋為何同樣序列、不同密碼子使用導致截然不同的蛋白表現量。

NMD (Nonsense-Mediated Decay): triggered when an EJC remains ≥ 50-55 nt downstream of the stop codon:

  1. Ribosome terminates at PTC
  2. SURF: SMG1 (PIKK kinase) + UPF1 + eRF1 + eRF3
  3. SMG1 phosphorylates UPF1 → DECID complex
  4. SMG6 endonuclease cleaves mRNA; SMG5-SMG7 recruit CCR4-NOT and DCP1/2
  5. XRN1 + exosome complete decay

Codon optimality (Coller 2014+): mRNA half-life depends on codon composition — common codons (high tRNA pools) → smooth translation → stable mRNA; rare codons → ribosome stalling → CCR4-NOT recruitment → deadenylation → decay. Explains why identical proteins from different codon-optimized sequences have vastly different expression.

自我檢核（3 題）

1. NMD 的「50-55 nt 規則」是指：

1. The NMD "50-55 nt rule" refers to:

A. mRNA 短於 50-55 nt 才會降解A. mRNAs shorter than 50-55 nt are degraded
B. stop codon 後方仍存在 EJC（距離下個 exon-exon junction ≥ 50-55 nt）即觸發 NMDB. NMD triggers if an EJC remains ≥ 50-55 nt downstream of the stop codon
C. polyA tail 短於 50-55 nt 觸發C. PolyA tail < 50-55 nt triggers NMD
D. 5′ UTR 須長於 50-55 ntD. 5′ UTR must exceed 50-55 nt
✔ B：EJC 於剪接後留在 exon-exon junction 上游 ~20 nt；ribosome 通過時會清除它。若 PTC 後方仍有 EJC，視為「異常」並觸發 NMD。
✔ B: EJCs deposit ~20 nt upstream of exon-exon junctions; the ribosome strips them off. A retained EJC downstream of a stop codon flags an aberrant mRNA, triggering NMD.

2. mRNA 疫苗為何使用 N1-methylpseudouridine (m1ψ) 取代 U？

2. Why do mRNA vaccines replace U with N1-methylpseudouridine (m1ψ)?

A. 減少 costA. Reduce cost
B. 加快轉錄B. Speed up transcription
C. 減少 TLR3/7/8 活化（innate immune evasion）並提升轉譯效率C. Reduce TLR3/7/8 activation (innate immune evasion) and boost translation efficiency
D. 增加 mRNA 抗原性D. Increase mRNA antigenicity
✔ C：m1ψ 避免 dsRNA-like TLR 訊號被觸發，避免 IFN-I 強反應，並使 ribosome 滑動更平順——這是 Karikó & Weissman 2023 諾貝爾獎核心發現。
✔ C: m1ψ evades dsRNA-like TLR signaling and IFN-I responses, plus smooths ribosome elongation — Karikó & Weissman 2023 Nobel discovery.

3. (T/F) Nanopore direct RNA-seq can simultaneously detect m6A sites, alternative splicing, and poly(A) tail length at the single-molecule level.
