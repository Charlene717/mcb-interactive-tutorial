ER-Golgi 與分泌途徑

從共轉譯入 ER、N-glycosylation、COPII/COPI 囊泡，到 Golgi 醣化階梯與 TGN 分揀——細胞的「物流系統」。

From co-translational ER entry, N-glycosylation, COPII/COPI vesicles, to the Golgi glycan ladder and TGN sorting — the cell's logistics network.

概覽

分泌流程

真核細胞約 1/3 的蛋白由分泌途徑合成：核糖體 → ER → ERES → cis-Golgi → medial → trans-Golgi → TGN → 細胞膜 / 內體 / 溶酶體 / 顆粒。每一站完成特定修飾與分揀。整個流程速度跨越 6 個數量級：

  • 共轉譯入 ER：< 1 秒 (translocon 開門)
  • ER 折疊質檢：~ 30 分鐘 (BiP/calnexin cycle)
  • ER → Golgi：~ 5-15 分鐘 (COPII vesicle 或 ERGIC tubule)
  • Golgi 醣化：~ 10-30 分鐘
  • 調節性分泌：可儲存數小時 (chromaffin granule)

當代視角：過去把 Golgi 視為「裝配線」，現在發現它更像動態 cisternal maturation——cisternae 本身會「老化」並改變酵素組成，配合 COPI 回收逆向運輸。

About one-third of eukaryotic proteins enter the secretory pathway: ribosome → ER → ERES → cis-Golgi → medial → trans-Golgi → TGN → PM / endosome / lysosome / granules. Each station performs specific modifications and sorting. Time scales span six orders of magnitude:

  • Co-translational ER entry: < 1 s (translocon gating)
  • ER quality control: ~ 30 min (BiP/calnexin cycle)
  • ER → Golgi: ~ 5-15 min (COPII vesicles or ERGIC tubules)
  • Golgi glycosylation: ~ 10-30 min
  • Regulated secretion: hours of storage (chromaffin granules)

Modern view: Golgi is not a static assembly line but a dynamic cisternal maturation system — cisternae age and change enzyme content, with COPI ensuring retrograde recycling.

入 ER

一、共轉譯入 ER

當新生肽含 N 端 signal sequence (15-25 aa, 含疏水核心) 自核糖體露出時，SRP (Signal Recognition Particle, 7SL RNA + 6 蛋白) 結合並使轉譯暫停。SRP 帶核糖體至 ER 膜 SR (SRP Receptor)，GTP 水解後將核糖體交給 Sec61 translocon。

Sec61 αβγ heterotrimer 是一道側面可開的「沙漏」：可向膜雙層側側向開門釋放跨膜 (TM) 螺旋，或保持「直通」讓可溶蛋白進入 ER lumen。Signal peptidase 切除 signal sequence；OST (Oligosaccharyltransferase) 將 Glc₃Man₉GlcNAc₂ 預組裝醣鏈轉移至 Asn-X-S/T 共識序列 — 這是 N-glycosylation 的起點。

2024 進展：Hegde / Voorhees 解析多種 translocon-ribosome-substrate 複合體 (~3 Å)，揭示TM 拓樸決定由 Sec61 lateral gate 的疏水力學決定；GET / EMC 補充負責 tail-anchored 與多次跨膜蛋白。

When a nascent chain exposes its N-terminal signal sequence (15-25 aa, hydrophobic core), SRP (7SL RNA + 6 proteins) binds and pauses translation. SRP brings the ribosome to the ER membrane SR (SRP Receptor); GTP hydrolysis hands off to Sec61 translocon.

The Sec61 αβγ heterotrimer is a side-gated hourglass: it can laterally open to release TM helices into the bilayer, or stay closed to thread soluble proteins into the lumen. Signal peptidase cleaves the signal; OST transfers the pre-assembled Glc₃Man₉GlcNAc₂ glycan onto Asn-X-S/T motifs — start of N-glycosylation.

2024: Hegde/Voorhees solved multiple translocon-ribosome-substrate complexes (~3 Å), showing TM topology is dictated by Sec61 lateral-gate hydrophobics; GET and EMC complexes handle tail-anchored and multipass proteins.

折疊質檢

二、ER 折疊質檢

ER lumen 是專業折疊環境 (Ca²⁺ 高, 氧化, ATP-driven chaperone)。新蛋白經 BiP (HSPA5) 與 calnexin/calreticulin (CNX/CRT) cycle 進行迭代折疊：

  1. OST 加 Glc₃Man₉GlcNAc₂ → glucosidase I/II 切兩個 Glc → Glc₁Man₉
  2. CNX/CRT 結合 Glc₁ 端 + ERp57 形成 disulfide
  3. Glucosidase II 切最後 Glc → 釋放
  4. 若折疊正確 → ER export
  5. 若折疊不正確 → UGGT (UDP-Glc:glycoprotein glucosyltransferase) 重新加 Glc → 回到 CNX/CRT (再試)
  6. 若多次失敗 → mannose 被 EDEM 切除 → 進入 ERAD

ERAD (ER-Associated Degradation)：misfolded 蛋白被 E3 ligase（Hrd1 主膜內 luminal, gp78 主膜上 cytosolic）標記，p97/VCP AAA+ ATPase 提供 ATP 力逆向運輸至質內，再由 26S 蛋白酶體降解。UPR 與 ERAD 容量耗盡時觸發 apoptosis (Ch8)。

ER lumen is a specialized folding environment (high Ca²⁺, oxidizing, ATP-driven chaperones). New proteins iterate through BiP and the calnexin/calreticulin (CNX/CRT) cycle:

  1. OST adds Glc₃Man₉GlcNAc₂ → glucosidase I/II trims two Glc → Glc₁Man₉
  2. CNX/CRT binds the Glc₁ end + ERp57 forms disulfides
  3. Glucosidase II removes last Glc → release
  4. If correctly folded → ER export
  5. If misfolded → UGGT re-adds Glc → back to CNX/CRT (retry)
  6. If repeatedly failing → EDEM trims mannose → enters ERAD

ERAD: misfolded proteins are tagged by E3 ligases (Hrd1 for luminal/membrane, gp78 for cytosolic-facing); p97/VCP AAA+ ATPase provides ATP-driven retrotranslocation to the cytosol, then 26S proteasome degrades them. When UPR + ERAD capacity is overwhelmed → apoptosis (Ch8).

囊泡塗層

三、COPII 與 COPI

COPII (ER → Golgi)：從 ERES 起始。Sar1-GEF Sec12 將 cytosolic Sar1-GDP 換成 Sar1-GTP → 暴露 N-端兩性螺旋插入 ER 膜 → 招募內層 Sec23/Sec24 dimer (Sec24 提供 cargo 結合槽，識別 DXE、YYM、LXXLE motifs) → 招募外層 Sec13/Sec31 lattice (cuboctahedral / icosahedral 籠子, ~60-80 nm vesicle)。

大型 cargo (procollagen ~300 nm) 的問題：無法塞進標準 60 nm COPII vesicle。解方：TANGO1 (MIA3) 將 procollagen 招募至 ERES，並透過 SH3 domain 連接 collagen + cTAGE5 + Sec23A，使 COPII 籠子延伸成管 (ERES tubule)。TANGO1 突變導致 carrocele、骨發育異常。

COPI (Golgi → ER 回收)：由七亞基 coatomer (α/β/β'/γ/δ/ε/ζ) + Arf1-GTP 組成。Arf1 由 GBF1 (cis-Golgi) 活化。負責 (1) ER-resident 蛋白（KKXX 信號）回收；(2) Golgi 內 cisternal maturation 的逆向轉運；(3) lipid droplet 形成。

COPII (ER → Golgi): starts at ERES. Sar1-GEF Sec12 swaps Sar1-GDP for Sar1-GTP → N-terminal amphipathic helix inserts into ER membrane → recruits inner layer Sec23/Sec24 (Sec24 has cargo-binding sites for DXE, YYM, LXXLE motifs) → recruits outer Sec13/Sec31 lattice (cuboctahedral/icosahedral cage, ~60-80 nm vesicle).

Large cargo problem (procollagen ~300 nm): too big for standard vesicles. Solution: TANGO1 (MIA3) recruits procollagen at ERES; via SH3 it links collagen + cTAGE5 + Sec23A, making the COPII cage extend into tubules. TANGO1 mutations cause carrocele and bone defects.

COPI (Golgi → ER retrograde): seven-subunit coatomer (α/β/β'/γ/δ/ε/ζ) + Arf1-GTP. Arf1 activated by GBF1 (cis-Golgi). Functions: (1) recycle ER-resident proteins (KKXX signal); (2) retrograde transport for cisternal maturation; (3) lipid droplet biogenesis.

⚠️
Cisternal maturation 證據 (Glick 2024): Live imaging 顯示 cis 酵素 (Mannosidase I) 隨 cisterna「老化」消失，被 trans 酵素 (Galactosyltransferase) 取代。COPI 持續回收較老酵素回到較新 cisternae——這是「囊泡逆流支持成熟」模型的核心。 Cisternal maturation (Glick 2024): Live imaging shows cis enzymes (Mannosidase I) disappear as cisternae age, replaced by trans enzymes (Galactosyltransferase). COPI continually recycles older enzymes back to younger cisternae — central to the "vesicle-supported maturation" model.
TGN

四、TGN 與分泌

TGN (Trans-Golgi Network) 是分揀總站：

  • Clathrin + AP-1：去後期內體 / 部分回到 PM；含 dileucine / YXXΦ 信號的 cargo
  • AP-3：去溶酶體相關胞器 (LRO, melanosome) 與部分溶酶體
  • AP-4：去軸突；缺失導致 SPG47-52 痙攣性截癱
  • M6P 受體：辨識 lysosomal 酵素的 mannose-6-phosphate → 經 AP-1/clathrin 至 late endosome
  • Constitutive secretion：無特定信號，預設出膜
  • Regulated secretion：分泌顆粒，aggregon-tagged cargo (chromogranin)，Ca²⁺ 觸發 (SNARE syt1)

Unconventional Protein Secretion (UCPS, 2024 review): 部分蛋白繞過 ER-Golgi 直接釋出（如 IL-1β、FGF2、Annexin A2、Galectins）。機制包含 GRASP-driven CUPS 結構、pore-mediated (gasdermin)、autophagy-based、direct membrane insertion。在發炎反應與癌中重要。

TGN is the master sorting hub:

  • Clathrin + AP-1: to late endosomes / partial PM return; dileucine / YXXΦ motif cargo
  • AP-3: to lysosome-related organelles (LROs, melanosomes) and some lysosomes
  • AP-4: to axons; loss → SPG47-52 spastic paraplegia
  • M6P receptor: recognizes M6P-tagged lysosomal enzymes → AP-1/clathrin → late endosome
  • Constitutive secretion: no specific signal, default PM delivery
  • Regulated secretion: secretory granules, aggregon-tagged cargo (chromogranin), Ca²⁺-triggered fusion (SNARE syt1)

Unconventional Protein Secretion (UCPS, 2024 review): some proteins bypass ER-Golgi (IL-1β, FGF2, Annexin A2, galectins). Mechanisms: GRASP-driven CUPS structures, pore-mediated (gasdermin), autophagy-based, direct membrane insertion. Critical in inflammation and cancer.
