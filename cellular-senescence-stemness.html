細胞衰老與幹性

從 SASP 與 SAHF 到 iPSC 與部分重編程——衰老/幹性軸如何決定組織老化、修復與再生。

From SASP and SAHF to iPSC and partial reprogramming — how the senescence-stemness axis dictates tissue aging, repair and regeneration.

概覽

同一張地圖的兩端

細胞衰老 (cellular senescence) 不是「老化的細胞學定義」那麼簡單——它是一種穩定的不可逆細胞週期停滯 + SASP 分泌 + 抗凋亡的三聯特徵。生理上有益（傷口修復、抑癌、胚胎發育），長期累積則有害（組織衰退、慢性發炎、惡性轉化促進）。

另一端的幹性 (stemness)：可自我更新且分化潛能。2006 Yamanaka 證明 OCT4/SOX2/KLF4/MYC 可將體細胞重編程為 iPSC，開啟了「細胞身分可塑」時代。當代核心問題：能否部分重編程逆轉衰老指標而不失去細胞身分？

Cellular senescence is not simply "aged cells" — it is a triad of stable irreversible cell-cycle arrest + SASP secretion + apoptosis resistance. Beneficial physiologically (wound healing, tumor suppression, embryogenesis); chronic accumulation drives tissue decline, inflammation, malignancy.

Stemness, the opposite pole: self-renewal + differentiation potential. In 2006 Yamanaka showed OCT4/SOX2/KLF4/MYC reprogram somatic cells to iPSC, launching the "cell identity is plastic" era. The current question: can partial reprogramming reverse aging markers without losing cell identity?

💡
SenNet & 老化十二大特徵 (López-Otín 2023)：NIH SenNet 聯盟正繪製「衰老圖譜」於多組織單細胞解析度（2024-2026）。López-Otín 2023 Cell 文擴展為 12 個 hallmarks of aging（見 Ch20）。 SenNet & 12 hallmarks of aging (López-Otín 2023): NIH SenNet consortium is mapping senescence at single-cell resolution across tissues (2024-2026). López-Otín's 2023 Cell paper expanded to 12 hallmarks of aging (see Ch20).

SASP 與療法

二、SASP 的矛盾性

🔥

SASP 組成

細胞激素：IL-6, IL-8, IL-1α/β；趨化因子：CXCL1, CCL2；MMPs：MMP3, MMP9；生長因子：IGFBP3, GDF15；外泌體：含 miRNA。受 NF-κB、C/EBPβ、p38-MAPK、mTOR、cGAS-STING 共調。

Cytokines: IL-6, IL-8, IL-1α/β; chemokines: CXCL1, CCL2; MMPs: MMP3/9; growth factors: IGFBP3, GDF15; EVs with miRNAs. Regulated by NF-κB, C/EBPβ, p38-MAPK, mTOR, cGAS-STING.

⚖️

利與弊

有益：傷口收口、清除前癌細胞、胚胎塑形、纖維化解析。有害：慢性發炎（inflammaging）、組織纖維化、促進附近細胞 senescence/transformation（旁分泌）、CAF-like 促進腫瘤。

Beneficial: wound closure, pre-malignant clearance, embryonic patterning, fibrosis resolution. Detrimental: inflammaging, tissue fibrosis, paracrine senescence/transformation of neighbors, CAF-like tumor promotion.

💊

療法

Senolytics 殺死衰老細胞：Dasatinib+Quercetin (D+Q)、Fisetin、Navitoclax (BCL-2/W/XL i)、UBX-1325（眼科 phase 2）。Senomorphics 壓制 SASP：Rapamycin (mTORi)、Metformin、JAK inhibitors。臨床試驗 IPF/糖尿病腎病/AD 進行中。

Senolytics kill senescent cells: Dasatinib+Quercetin (D+Q), Fisetin, Navitoclax (BCL-2/W/XLi), UBX-1325 (ophthalmology phase 2). Senomorphics suppress SASP: Rapamycin (mTORi), Metformin, JAK inhibitors. IPF / diabetic kidney / AD trials ongoing.

幹性與重編程

三、幹性核心 + 部分重編程

🌳 細胞身分可塑性

基準→多能性核心：OCT4-SOX2-NANOG triumvirate + KLF4, MYC, ESRRB, TBX3——維持 ES/iPSC 自更新與分化潛能Pluripotency core: OCT4-SOX2-NANOG triumvirate + KLF4, MYC, ESRRB, TBX3 — maintain ES/iPSC self-renewal and differentiation potential
完全重編程→體細胞 + OSKM (Yamanaka 因子) → iPSC（30-60 天，過程混亂、效率低；含 epigenetic memory）Somatic + OSKM (Yamanaka) → iPSC (30-60 days, inefficient; retains epigenetic memory)
部分重編程→短期 OSK 表達（去除 MYC）→ 抹除部分 epigenetic age（Horvath clock 倒退）卻不去身分。Belmonte 2016/2020、Sinclair 2020 視神經、Altos Labs/Retro Bio 試驗Brief OSK pulse (no MYC) → erases part of epigenetic age (Horvath clock reversal) without losing identity. Belmonte 2016/2020, Sinclair 2020 (optic nerve), Altos Labs/Retro Bio trials
直接重編程→轉錄因子直接轉換身分：Sox2/Pax6/Brn2/Mash1 → 神經元；GATA4/Mef2c/Tbx5 → 心肌；不經 pluripotent 中介TFs directly switch identity: Sox2/Pax6/Brn2/Mash1 → neurons; GATA4/Mef2c/Tbx5 → cardiomyocytes; no pluripotent intermediate
類器官 (Organoid)→Sasai/Clevers/Lancaster：iPSC/成體幹細胞 + 3D matrix + morphogen → 自組織腸 / 腦 / 腎 / 肝 / 視網膜類器官；Assembloids 整合多胞層Sasai/Clevers/Lancaster: iPSC or adult SC + 3D matrix + morphogens → self-organized intestinal/brain/kidney/liver/retinal organoids; assembloids integrate multi-lineage
⚠️
表觀遺傳時鐘 (Horvath clock)：基於 ~353 CpG 甲基化的迴歸模型，可預測實際生物年齡，相關於壽命/死亡率。部分重編程的關鍵驗證指標即「epigenetic age reversal」——但「epigenetic age = biological age」仍有爭議。 Horvath clock: regression on ~353 CpG methylations predicts actual biological age, correlating with lifespan/mortality. Key validation for partial reprogramming is "epigenetic age reversal" — but "epigenetic age = biological age" remains debated.

common = coef.index.intersection(beta.index)
# 2) 線性組合 + Horvath 變換
score = (beta.loc[common].T @ coef.loc[common,"coef"]) + coef.loc["intercept","coef"]

自我檢核（3 題）

1. 為什麼僅以 SA-β-Gal 單一染色判定細胞衰老並不嚴謹？

1. Why is SA-β-Gal alone insufficient to call a cell senescent?

A. SA-β-Gal 只在分裂中細胞表現A. SA-β-Gal is only in dividing cells
B. SA-β-Gal 對 p21 不敏感B. SA-β-Gal is p21-insensitive
C. SA-β-Gal 可被高融合度或溶酶體擴大造成假陽性；需多 marker (p16/p21/Lamin B1↓/SAHF) 共同判定C. SA-β-Gal yields false positives with high confluence or enlarged lysosomes; combined markers (p16/p21/Lamin B1↓/SAHF) required
D. SA-β-Gal 僅可在組織切片使用D. SA-β-Gal only works in tissue sections
✔ C：SA-β-Gal 為 pH6 的酸性 β-Gal 活性，因溶酶體擴大可在非衰老細胞陽性；當代研究要求多 marker 共驗。
✔ C: SA-β-Gal reflects pH6 β-Gal activity from enlarged lysosomes, positive in non-senescent cells. Modern studies require multi-marker validation.

2. 部分重編程 (OSK pulse) 為何優於完全 OSKM 重編程作為「逆轉衰老」策略？

2. Why is partial reprogramming (OSK pulse) preferred over full OSKM reprogramming as a "senescence reversal" strategy?

A. OSK 更便宜A. OSK is cheaper
B. 短暫表達抹除部分 epigenetic age 又不喪失細胞身分；去除 MYC 降低致瘤性B. Transient expression erases epigenetic age without losing identity; removing MYC reduces tumorigenicity
C. 完全重編程已被證明不可能C. Full reprogramming has been shown impossible
D. OSK 只活化 senolytic 基因D. OSK only activates senolytic genes
✔ B：完整 iPSC 化會喪失組織身分且過程致瘤；OSK 短脈衝倒退 Horvath 鐘卻保留細胞分化狀態。
✔ B: Full iPSC reprogramming erases tissue identity and is tumorigenic; OSK pulse rewinds the Horvath clock while preserving lineage.

3.（是非）Dasatinib + Quercetin (D+Q) 屬於 senolytics——透過誘導衰老細胞凋亡來改善老化表型；而 rapamycin 屬於 senomorphics——主要壓制 SASP 而不殺死細胞。

3. (T/F) Dasatinib + Quercetin (D+Q) is a senolytic — inducing apoptosis in senescent cells; rapamycin is a senomorphic — suppressing SASP without killing senescent cells.

T
F
✔ T：兩類介入機制不同——senolytics 標靶 SCAP（survival pathway，如 BCL-2/W）；senomorphics 抑 mTOR/NF-κB 等 SASP 上游。
✔ T: Different mechanisms — senolytics target SCAPs (survival pathways like BCL-2/W); senomorphics suppress mTOR/NF-κB upstream of SASP.
