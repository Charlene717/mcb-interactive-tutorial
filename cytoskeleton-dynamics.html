細胞骨架動力學

Actin 跑步機、微管動態不穩、中間絲韌性——三大細胞骨架如何協同編排細胞的形狀、運動與分裂。

Actin treadmilling, microtubule instability, intermediate filament resilience — how three cytoskeletons choreograph shape, motility, and division.

概覽

三大骨架

細胞骨架不是死板的「骨架」，而是持續組裝/解構的動態網路。每種骨架由其單體幾何與聚合熱力學決定了功能：

  • Actin (~7 nm)：細、柔、極性、ATP 水解驅動 treadmilling。負責 lamellipodia、filopodia、cytokinesis、cortical tension。Persistence length 17 μm。
  • Microtubules (~25 nm)：粗、剛、極性、GTP 水解驅動 dynamic instability。負責有絲分裂紡錘體、長距離運輸、initial polarity。Persistence length 5 mm。
  • Intermediate filaments (~10 nm)：無極性、無 ATP/GTP、極強拉伸韌性。負責機械整合 (lamins, vimentin, keratin, GFAP)。Persistence length 1 μm，可拉伸至 3.5×。

Not rigid scaffolds but continuously assembling/disassembling networks. Subunit geometry and polymerization thermodynamics dictate function:

  • Actin (~7 nm): thin, flexible, polar, ATP-driven treadmilling. Lamellipodia, filopodia, cytokinesis, cortical tension. Persistence length 17 μm.
  • Microtubules (~25 nm): thick, rigid, polar, GTP-driven dynamic instability. Mitotic spindle, long-distance transport. Persistence length 5 mm.
  • Intermediate filaments (~10 nm): apolar, no nucleotide, extreme tensile resilience. Mechanical integration (lamins, vimentin, keratin, GFAP). Persistence length 1 μm; stretches 3.5×.

Actin 動力學

一、Actin treadmilling

Treadmilling：G-actin-ATP 在 + 端 (barbed) 聚合 (K_d ≈ 0.1 μM)，水解 ATP→ADP，從 − 端 (pointed) 解離 (K_d ≈ 0.6 μM)。當胞漿 G-actin 濃度介於兩 K_d 之間 (約 0.2 μM)，纖維淨向 + 端「跑」——這是 lamellipodia 前緣推進的物理基礎。

關鍵調控者：

  • Profilin：螯合 G-actin-ATP，催化 ADP→ATP 交換，並將 actin 送至 formin / WASP
  • Arp2/3 複合體：在母絲側面 70° 分支成核——形成 lamellipodia 的樹枝狀網路 (Mullins/Pollard)
  • Formins (mDia1, FMNL2)：FH2 環抱 + 端，FH1 招募 profilin-actin → 形成無分支的長纖維 (filopodia, contractile ring)
  • Capping protein (CapZ)：+ 端封端、防止過長
  • ADF/Cofilin：偏愛 ADP-actin，加速 − 端解離；高濃度可切斷纖維
  • WAVE / WASP / N-WASP：將 Rac1/Cdc42 訊號轉為 Arp2/3 活化

Treadmilling: G-actin-ATP polymerizes at the (+) barbed end (K_d ≈ 0.1 μM), hydrolyzes ATP→ADP, and dissociates from the (−) pointed end (K_d ≈ 0.6 μM). When cytosolic G-actin sits between the two K_d (~0.2 μM), the filament net-translocates toward (+) — the physical basis of lamellipodial protrusion.

Key regulators:

  • Profilin: sequesters G-actin-ATP, catalyzes ADP→ATP exchange, delivers actin to formin/WASP
  • Arp2/3: 70° side-branch nucleation — dendritic network (Mullins/Pollard)
  • Formins (mDia1, FMNL2): FH2 wraps the (+) end; FH1 recruits profilin-actin → unbranched long filaments
  • Capping protein (CapZ): caps (+) end, limits length
  • ADF/Cofilin: prefers ADP-actin, accelerates (−)-end loss; at high density, severs filaments
  • WAVE / WASP / N-WASP: translate Rac1/Cdc42 signals into Arp2/3 activation
💡
2024 CryoEM：Sept-Mullins 解析 actin-Arp2/3 分支介面 (3.4 Å)，揭示 Arp2 與母絲第 12-13 subunit 的特定接觸——這是設計 branch-disrupting 化合物 (CK-666 改良) 的結構基礎。 2024 CryoEM: Sept-Mullins solved the actin-Arp2/3 branch interface (3.4 Å), revealing specific Arp2 contacts with mother filament subunits 12-13 — a structural basis for next-gen CK-666 analogs.
微管動態

二、微管動態不穩

微管由 αβ-tubulin 異二聚體頭尾聚合成 protofilament，13 條 protofilament 圍成中空管。GTP-cap 模型：+ 端富 GTP-tubulin 時穩定生長；GTP 水解後 GDP-tubulin 易解離——若 cap 失守，發生 catastrophe 快速縮短 (~20 μm/min)；GTP-tubulin 重新吸附則 rescue。這種「不穩」是探索空間的策略 (search & capture)。

+TIPs (Plus-end tracking proteins)：EB1/EB3 是核心——它們偏好 GTP-cap，再招募 CLIP-170、CLASP、p150-glued、APC、ch-TOG。EB1 在活細胞下呈彗星狀 (comet)，是觀察 MT 動態的金標準。

Tubulin code (2024 更新)：tubulin 經歷多種 PTM——acetylation (αK40, 由 αTAT1)、detyrosination (CCP enzymes 切 αE439)、polyglutamylation (TTLL 家族於 E 側鏈加 5-15 個 Glu)、polyglycylation。這些 PTM 重塑 motor 行為：detyrosination 使 kinesin-1 偏好特定 MT、polyglutamylation 抑制 spastin/katanin 切割（過量則促進切割——「dosage code」）。

MTs are head-to-tail αβ-tubulin polymers; 13 protofilaments form a hollow tube. GTP-cap model: (+) end with GTP-tubulin grows stably; after GTP→GDP hydrolysis, GDP-tubulin readily dissociates. Losing the cap triggers catastrophe (rapid shrinkage ~20 μm/min); GTP-tubulin re-addition triggers rescue. This instability is a spatial-search strategy (search & capture).

+TIPs: EB1/EB3 prefer the GTP-cap and recruit CLIP-170, CLASP, p150-glued, APC, ch-TOG. In live cells, EB1 appears as comets — gold standard for MT dynamics imaging.

Tubulin code (2024 update): tubulin undergoes acetylation (αK40, αTAT1), detyrosination (CCPs cleave αE439), polyglutamylation (TTLLs add 5-15 Glu to side chains), polyglycylation. PTMs reshape motor behavior: detyrosination biases kinesin-1; moderate polyglutamylation inhibits spastin/katanin (high amounts promote severing — "dosage code").

Katanin / Spastin

AAA+ ATPase，在 polyglu PTM 處切斷 MT，產生新 + 端與短片段——神經元軸突 MT 多樣性關鍵。

AAA+ ATPases sever MTs at polyglu PTM sites — generate new (+) ends, drive axonal MT diversity.

🚂

Kinesin

45+ 種；多數朝 + 端 (kinesin-1, -3, -5)；kinesin-14 (Ncd) 朝 − 端。Step 8 nm 每 ATP。

45+ types; most (+)-directed (kinesin-1, -3, -5); kinesin-14 (Ncd) is (−)-directed. 8 nm step per ATP.

Cytoplasmic dynein

朝 − 端；與 dynactin + adaptor (BicD, Hook3, Spindly) 形成活化複合體；2024 CryoEM 解析啟動機制。

(−)-directed; activated by dynactin + adaptors (BicD, Hook3, Spindly). 2024 CryoEM solved activation mechanism.

中間絲

三、中間絲、septins、纖毛

中間絲 (IF) 由 ~70 種基因編碼，依組織分類：keratin (上皮)、vimentin (間質)、desmin (肌肉)、neurofilament (神經元 NF-L/M/H)、GFAP (星狀膠細胞)、lamin A/B/C (核纖層)。每單體含 α-helix rod (含 4 個 coil) + 頭尾無序區，antiparallel 二聚體 → 反向四聚體 → 32-mer ULF → 成熟絲。IF 不耗能、不極性，但可拉伸至 3.5× 原長——是力學緩衝角色。Vimentin 在 EMT 與癌轉移大量上調。

Septins：第四種細胞骨架成員（GTP-binding 多聚體）。SEPT2-6-7-9 等以六/八聚體環狀組裝，在細胞質分裂環、樹突棘、ciliary base 形成 diffusion barrier。Mostowy 2024：septin 也在 actin-membrane 介面充當壓力感應器。

Cilia (primary cilium): 由 9+0 或 9+2 axoneme 組成，依賴 IFT (intraflagellar transport) — kinesin-2 (KIF3A/B/KAP3) 將 IFT-B 顆粒運向 + 端 tip; dynein-2 將 IFT-A 帶回 base。Hedgehog 訊號通路 (Smo, Gli) 在 primary cilium 內運作——ciliopathies 包含 BBS、Joubert、Meckel。

IFs: ~70 genes, tissue-specific — keratin (epithelial), vimentin (mesenchymal), desmin (muscle), neurofilament NF-L/M/H, GFAP (astrocyte), lamins A/B/C (nuclear). Monomer = α-helix rod (4 coils) + disordered head/tail. Antiparallel dimer → tetramer → 32-mer ULF → mature filament. Energy-independent, apolar, but stretchable to 3.5× original length — mechanical buffering. Vimentin is markedly upregulated in EMT and metastasis.

Septins: the fourth cytoskeleton (GTP-binding polymers). SEPT2-6-7-9 form hexameric/octameric rings at cytokinetic furrows, dendritic spines, and the ciliary base — diffusion barriers. Mostowy (2024): septins also act as pressure sensors at the actin-membrane interface.

Primary cilium: 9+0 or 9+2 axoneme, powered by IFT — kinesin-2 (KIF3A/B/KAP3) carries IFT-B to the tip; dynein-2 returns IFT-A. Hedgehog signaling (Smo, Gli) runs through the primary cilium — ciliopathies include BBS, Joubert, Meckel syndromes.

# EB1 comet 追蹤 — trackpy + matplotlib
import trackpy as tp
import pims, numpy as np
frames = pims.open("eb1_gfp.tif")
# 1) per-frame feature detection (size = comet 直徑 px)
f = tp.batch(frames, diameter=5, minmass=300, percentile=90)
# 2) 鏈接 trajectories
t = tp.link(f, search_range=3, memory=2)
t = tp.filter_stubs(t, threshold=5)
# 3) 計算 + 端生長速率 (μm/min)
t["vx"] = t.groupby("particle")["x"].diff()
t["vy"] = t.groupby("particle")["y"].diff()
v = np.sqrt(t.vx**2 + t.vy**2) * 0.108 * 60 / dt_sec  # μm/min
print(f"Mean MT growth: {v.mean():.2f} μm/min")
# Healthy mammalian cell: 10-25 μm/min
# Actin / MT 動力學摘要分析 (R)
library(tidyverse); library(survival)
df <- read_csv("mt_tracks.csv")
# Catastrophe 頻率 = 1 / mean lifetime in growth phase
df <- df %>% group_by(track_id) %>%
  mutate(state = case_when(speed > 0.5 ~ "growth",
                          speed < -1 ~ "shrink",
                          TRUE ~ "pause"))
# Survival analysis on growth-phase lifetimes
growth <- df %>% filter(state == "growth") %>%
  group_by(track_id) %>% summarise(t = max(time) - min(time), event=1)
fit <- survfit(Surv(t, event) ~ 1, data=growth)
print(fit)  # median growth time → catastrophe frequency
