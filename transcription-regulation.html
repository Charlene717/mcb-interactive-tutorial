轉錄調控當代圖像

Pol II CTD 循環、promoter-proximal pausing、Mediator-BRD4 凝聚物——當代轉錄不是線性過程而是動態相分離工廠。

Pol II CTD cycle, promoter-proximal pausing, Mediator-BRD4 condensates — modern transcription is not linear but a dynamic phase-separated factory.

概覽

從線性到凝聚物

傳統教科書描繪轉錄為「TF 結合 → 招募 Pol II → 起始 → 延伸 → 終止」的線性流程。今日已知這幅圖過於簡化——當代轉錄涉及：

  • Pol II CTD 翻譯後修飾循環：YSPTSPS 重複 52 次的 CTD 在 Ser5P→Ser2P 之間切換，協調 capping、splicing、3′ 加工
  • Promoter-proximal pausing：~80% 哺乳類基因 Pol II 啟動 30-60 bp 後即停頓，等待釋放訊號
  • 轉錄爆發 (transcriptional bursting)：每個基因以隨機的開/關週期表達，由 enhancer-promoter 接觸機率決定
  • 轉錄凝聚物：Mediator + BRD4 + Pol II 在 super-enhancer 上形成 LLPS droplets
  • TF clustering：TF 的 IDR-AD 透過 multivalent 弱交互作用聚集 (Chong et al. 2018)

Classical textbooks describe transcription as "TF → recruit Pol II → initiate → elongate → terminate" — a linear cascade. Today we know this is too simple. Modern transcription involves:

  • Pol II CTD PTM cycle: the 52× YSPTSPS repeat cycles between Ser5P→Ser2P, coordinating capping, splicing, 3′-end processing
  • Promoter-proximal pausing: ~80% of mammalian Pol II pauses 30-60 bp downstream of TSS, awaiting release
  • Transcriptional bursting: stochastic on/off cycles set by enhancer-promoter contact probability
  • Transcription condensates: Mediator + BRD4 + Pol II form LLPS droplets at super-enhancers
  • TF clustering: TF IDR-ADs cluster via multivalent weak interactions (Chong et al. 2018)
💡
核心反思：Young & Sharp 等 2017-2024 工作確立「轉錄即凝聚物」典範——enhancer-promoter loop 帶來物理接近，凝聚物提供高局部濃度的 Pol II 與輔活化子，這比單純的「招募」概念更貼近實況。 Core reframe: Young & Sharp 2017-2024 established the "transcription-as-condensate" paradigm. E-P loops bring physical proximity; condensates supply high local concentrations of Pol II and coactivators — beyond the simple "recruitment" model.
CTD 循環

一、Pol II CTD 循環

人類 Pol II RPB1 的 C-terminal domain (CTD) 含 52 個 YSPTSPS 重複。每個位置都可被磷酸化、糖基化或順反異構化——形成「CTD code」。最關鍵為 Ser5 與 Ser2 的時序性磷酸化：

⚠️
凝聚物切換：2018 Boehning et al. 發現 Ser5P CTD 偏好進入 Mediator/BRD4 凝聚物（起始）；Ser2P CTD 則偏好進入 splicing condensates（延伸）。CTD 磷酸化等同於改變 Pol II 在凝聚物間的「partition coefficient」。 Condensate switching: Boehning et al. 2018 — Ser5P CTD preferentially enters Mediator/BRD4 condensates (initiation); Ser2P CTD enters splicing condensates (elongation). CTD phosphorylation effectively changes Pol II's partition coefficient between condensates.
停頓釋放

二、停頓與釋放

Pol II 啟動轉錄後僅延伸 30-60 nt 即被「停頓 (pausing)」，由兩個複合體穩定：

  • NELF (Negative Elongation Factor)：NELF-A/B/C/D/E 抑制 Pol II 移動
  • DSIF (DRB-Sensitivity-Inducing Factor)：Spt4-Spt5，在停頓時為負調節，釋放後改為正調節

P-TEFb (CDK9-Cyclin T1) 是釋放的核心：

  1. P-TEFb 平常被 7SK snRNP（7SK RNA + HEXIM1 + LARP7 + MEPCE）封存（>80% 處於失活）
  2. 受訊號（如 BRD4 結合 H3K27ac, JMJD6, 或熱休克）招募，從 7SK snRNP 釋出
  3. P-TEFb 磷酸化 NELF（解離）、DSIF Spt5（變正向）、Pol II CTD Ser2
  4. Pol II 進入有效延伸

After ~30-60 nt of transcription, Pol II is paused, stabilized by two complexes:

  • NELF: NELF-A/B/C/D/E inhibits Pol II movement
  • DSIF: Spt4-Spt5 — negative during pause, positive after release

P-TEFb (CDK9-Cyclin T1) drives release:

  1. P-TEFb is normally sequestered (>80%) by 7SK snRNP (7SK RNA + HEXIM1 + LARP7 + MEPCE)
  2. Released on signal (BRD4 binding H3K27ac, JMJD6, heat shock)
  3. P-TEFb phosphorylates NELF (dissociation), DSIF Spt5 (→ positive), Pol II CTD Ser2
  4. Pol II enters productive elongation

① Ensures 5′ capping ② Rapid signal response ③ Prevents cryptic initiation ④ Maintains open chromatin

超級增強子

三、SE 與凝聚物

Super-enhancer (SE)（Whyte & Young 2013）是 H3K27ac、Mediator、BRD4 顯著富集的延伸基因組區段（>8-20 kb），常與細胞身分基因 (lineage TFs) 關聯。SE 的關鍵特性：

  • 協同效應：多個 enhancer cluster 之間出現非線性轉錄輸出（"transcription condensate" 解釋此現象）
  • 對劑量敏感：BRD4i (JQ1) 對 SE 比 typical enhancer 敏感 10-100×，是抗 MYC AML 治療基礎
  • 細胞身分定義：每種細胞約有 ~500 個 SE，唯一定義其身分（pluripotency: OCT4/SOX2/NANOG SEs）

Sabari et al. 2018 (Young lab): BRD4 與 MED1 含長 IDR，在 SE 上形成 1-2 µm 的 LLPS droplets，FRAP 顯示動態交換。Pol II Ser5P CTD 被招募進入此凝聚物。

Super-enhancer (SE) (Whyte & Young 2013): extended genomic regions (>8-20 kb) heavily decorated with H3K27ac, Mediator, BRD4, typically linked to lineage TFs. Key features:

  • Cooperativity: nonlinear output from clustered enhancers (explained by transcription condensates)
  • Dose sensitivity: BRD4i (JQ1) hits SEs 10-100× more than typical enhancers — basis of anti-MYC AML therapy
  • Identity definition: each cell type has ~500 SEs uniquely defining identity (pluripotency: OCT4/SOX2/NANOG SEs)

Sabari et al. 2018 (Young lab): BRD4 and MED1 contain long IDRs, forming 1-2 µm LLPS droplets at SEs with FRAP-confirmed dynamic exchange. Pol II Ser5P CTD partitions into these condensates.

Pioneer 因子

五、先導因子與 Mediator

Pioneer TFs（如 FOXA1, OCT4, KLF4, GATA1）能在封閉染色質上識別其結合位點——多數 TF 只能結合已開放的位點，但 pioneer 因子能穿透核小體，作為染色質開放的第一步。它們的特徵：

  • DBD 形似 linker histone (FOXA1 形如 H1)，能接觸纏繞於核小體的 DNA
  • 結合後招募 BAF (SWI/SNF)、p300/CBP，將 H3K27ac 加上
  • 細胞重編程關鍵：山中因子 OCT4/SOX2/KLF4/MYC 中 OCT4 與 KLF4 為 pioneer

Mediator complex（~30 個亞基，1.5 MDa）是 TF 與 Pol II 之間的「橋樑」：

  • Head + Middle：核心轉錄輸出
  • Tail：MED1, MED15 等含長 IDR，直接接觸 TF AD；MED1 IDR 是 SE 凝聚物的關鍵 scaffold
  • Kinase module (CDK8/CDK19)：可逆性結合，調控啟動效率

Pioneer TFs (FOXA1, OCT4, KLF4, GATA1) bind closed chromatin — most TFs require open sites, but pioneers penetrate nucleosomes as the first step in opening chromatin. Features:

  • DBD resembles linker histone (FOXA1 ≈ H1); contacts nucleosomal DNA
  • Recruits BAF (SWI/SNF), p300/CBP → H3K27ac
  • Reprogramming: among Yamanaka factors OCT4 & KLF4 are pioneers

Mediator complex (~30 subunits, 1.5 MDa): bridge between TF and Pol II:

  • Head + Middle: core transcription output
  • Tail: MED1, MED15 with long IDRs contact TF ADs; MED1 IDR is the key SE-condensate scaffold
  • Kinase module (CDK8/CDK19): reversibly associated; regulates initiation efficiency
