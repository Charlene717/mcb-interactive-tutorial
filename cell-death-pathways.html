調節型細胞死亡途徑

從凋亡到 ferroptosis、cuproptosis 與 PANoptosis——當代細胞死亡網絡的機制圖譜與療法。

From apoptosis to ferroptosis, cuproptosis and PANoptosis — the mechanistic map of modern cell death and therapeutics.

概覽

細胞死亡分子多元宇宙

細胞死亡命名委員會 (NCCD 2024) 已認可超過 20 種調節型細胞死亡 (RCD)。它們在形態、致死訊號、執行者、免疫後果都不同。理解這些途徑對腫瘤治療、神經退化、感染與發炎疾病的開發至為關鍵。

核心對比：apoptosis 為「無聲」死亡（不破膜、免疫沉默）；necroptosis/pyroptosis/ferroptosis 為「發炎」死亡（釋放 DAMPs，啟動免疫）。腫瘤學近期策略：誘導發炎性死亡以「加熱」冷腫瘤、激發 T 細胞反應。

NCCD 2024 recognizes > 20 regulated cell death (RCD) modalities, distinct in morphology, trigger, executioner, and immunogenicity. Understanding them is critical for oncology, neurodegeneration, infection and inflammation therapeutics.

Core contrast: apoptosis is "silent" (membrane intact, immune silent); necroptosis/pyroptosis/ferroptosis are "inflammatory" (release DAMPs, prime immunity). Current oncology aims to induce inflammatory death to "heat up" cold tumors and prime T cell responses.

💡
2023 PANoptosis：新證據顯示 pyroptosis、apoptosis、necroptosis 並非相互排斥，而是透過 PANoptosome（ZBP1-NLRP3-RIPK3-Caspase8 巨型複合體）整合的共通網絡——一個複合體同時驅動三種死亡。 2023 PANoptosis: emerging evidence — pyroptosis, apoptosis and necroptosis are not mutually exclusive but integrated via the PANoptosome (ZBP1-NLRP3-RIPK3-Caspase8 supramolecular complex) — one complex driving all three.
凋亡內外途徑

一、Apoptosis 兩條主軸

🧬

內在途徑（粒線體）

BH3-only 感應器 (BID, BIM, PUMA, NOXA, BAD, BMF) 偵測 DNA 損傷、ER 壓力、生長因子缺乏 → 拮抗 BCL-2/MCL-1/BCL-XL → 釋放 BAX/BAK 寡聚化於 OMM → MOMP (mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilization) → 釋出 cytochrome c + SMAC/Diablo + Omi/HtrA2 → 與 APAF-1 + Caspase-9 組裝Apoptosome → 切割 caspase-3/7。

BH3-only sensors (BID, BIM, PUMA, NOXA, BAD, BMF) detect DNA damage, ER stress, growth-factor loss → antagonize BCL-2/MCL-1/BCL-XL → release BAX/BAK oligomerization at OMM → MOMP → cytochrome c + SMAC + Omi → assemble Apoptosome with APAF-1 + Caspase-9 → cleave caspase-3/7.

🪝

外在途徑（死亡受體）

Fas / TRAILR1/2 / TNFR1 → DISC (FADD + procaspase-8/10) → caspase-8 活化 →（Type I）直接切 caspase-3；（Type II）切 BID → tBID → 觸發內在途徑放大訊號。cFLIP 競爭性抑制 caspase-8；cIAP/XIAP 抑制 caspase-3/7/9 (被 SMAC 拮抗)。

Fas / TRAILR / TNFR1 → DISC (FADD + procaspase-8/10) → caspase-8 active → (Type I) cleave caspase-3 directly; (Type II) cleave BID → tBID → amplify via intrinsic pathway. cFLIP competitively inhibits caspase-8; cIAP/XIAP inhibit caspase-3/7/9 (antagonized by SMAC).

⚠️
Venetoclax (ABT-199)：選擇性 BCL-2 抑制劑（BH3-mimetic）——2016 FDA 核准用於 CLL/AML。其作用機制即模擬 BH3-only 蛋白，釋放 BAX/BAK。Navitoclax (ABT-263) 同時抑 BCL-2/W/XL，但因 BCL-XL 抑制導致血小板減少。 Venetoclax (ABT-199): selective BCL-2 inhibitor (BH3-mimetic) — FDA approved 2016 for CLL/AML. Mechanism: mimics BH3-only proteins to release BAX/BAK. Navitoclax (ABT-263) targets BCL-2/W/XL but causes thrombocytopenia (BCL-XL inhibition).

Apoptosis-to-Pyroptosis 切換：Caspase-3 可切 Gasdermin E (GSDME)，將「沉默」凋亡轉為「發炎」pyroptosis——這在化療誘導死亡的免疫原性極為關鍵（2020 Wang et al., Nature）。ZBP1 感應內源 Z-RNA (見 Ch2 Z-DNA) → 招募 RIPK3 → necroptosis 是另一受關注的「核酸→死亡」軸。

Apoptosis-to-Pyroptosis switch: Caspase-3 cleaves Gasdermin E (GSDME), converting "silent" apoptosis to "inflammatory" pyroptosis — pivotal for chemo-induced immunogenicity (Wang et al., Nature 2020). ZBP1 senses endogenous Z-RNA (see Ch2 Z-DNA) → recruits RIPK3 → necroptosis is another spotlighted "nucleic-acid → death" axis.

金屬與脂質死亡

三、Ferroptosis & Cuproptosis

🩸

Ferroptosis

鐵依賴脂質過氧化。GPX4（含硒半胱胺酸）為主要防禦——還原 PL-OOH → PL-OH。失效時 PUFA 在 ACSL4-LPCAT3-LOX 軸被氧化 → 膜爆裂。Backup：FSP1-CoQ10/BH4（Bersuker/Doll 2019）、GCH1-BH4。誘導劑：RSL3 (GPX4i)、erastin (xCT-system Xc⁻)。

Iron-dependent lipid peroxidation. GPX4 (selenocysteine) reduces PL-OOH → PL-OH. When it fails, PUFAs are oxidized by ACSL4-LPCAT3-LOX → membrane rupture. Backups: FSP1-CoQ10/BH4 (Bersuker/Doll 2019), GCH1-BH4. Inducers: RSL3 (GPX4i), erastin (xCT-system Xc⁻).

🟠

Cuproptosis (2022)

Tsvetkov et al., Science 2022。Cu²⁺ 經 FDX1 還原為 Cu⁺ → 結合 TCA 循環的 lipoylated DLAT/DLST → 蛋白寡聚與毒性聚集 + Fe-S cluster 失常 → 蛋白毒性死亡。誘導劑 elesclomol-Cu。對線粒體呼吸依賴細胞特別敏感。

Tsvetkov et al., Science 2022. Cu²⁺ reduced to Cu⁺ by FDX1 → binds lipoylated DLAT/DLST of TCA → toxic oligomerization + Fe-S cluster loss → proteotoxic death. Inducer: elesclomol-Cu. Especially deadly to OXPHOS-dependent cells.

⚡

Parthanatos / Other

Parthanatos：PARP1 過度活化 → PAR polymer → AIF 釋放 → 核斷裂。Entosis：cell-in-cell；NETosis：嗜中性球 DNA 網；Lysosomal cell death：cathepsin 釋放 → MOMP。

Parthanatos: PARP1 hyperactivation → PAR polymer → AIF release → nuclear fragmentation. Entosis: cell-in-cell; NETosis: neutrophil DNA traps; Lysosomal cell death: cathepsin release → MOMP.

A. 高表達 BAK，凋亡門檻低A. High BAK expression lowers apoptosis threshold

D. cFLIP 高表達D. cFLIP overexpression
✔ B：被「primed for death」的細胞 BIM 已活化但被 BCL-2 隔離；venetoclax 釋放 BIM 即啟動 BAX/BAK MOMP。
✔ B: "Primed-for-death" cells have BIM activated but sequestered by BCL-2; venetoclax releases BIM, triggering BAX/BAK MOMP.
